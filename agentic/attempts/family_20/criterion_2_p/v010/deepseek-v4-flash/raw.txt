We can model this as a bipartite graph edge-coloring problem.  
The peak concentration is the maximum degree in the graph.  
Since the graph is bipartite, by König’s line coloring theorem, we can color the edges with exactly Δ colors, where Δ is the maximum degree.

We can use a simple greedy algorithm that works in O(m·Δ) worst-case, but with Δ up to 400000 that’s too slow.
