We can model this as an edge-coloring problem on a bipartite graph, where each test is an edge between a generator and an analyzer.  
The maximum degree \( K \) is the minimum number of colors (phases) needed.  

We can use a greedy edge-coloring algorithm that works in \( O(m \log m) \) or \( O(m) \) with careful handling.  
Since the graph is bipartite, we can color edges with exactly \( K \) colors using a simple algorithm:  
- For each edge, find a free color for its two endpoints, and if there’s a conflict, perform a Kempe chain recolor.

Here’s the implementation:
