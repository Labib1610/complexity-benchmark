Approach: For each connected component, bipartite graph. Two colorings (swap 1 and 2). We can choose assignment that gives lexicographically minimal overall. Since components are independent, we can decide per component. Lexicographic order: compare sequences from left to right. So we can process nodes in order 1..n, find component of node.
