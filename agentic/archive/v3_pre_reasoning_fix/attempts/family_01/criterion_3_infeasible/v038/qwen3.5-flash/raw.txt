In other words, we need to find a triangle (a clique of size 3) in the graph that is lexicographically smallest. We are guaranteed that at least one such triangle exists.

The constraints are $n \le 2000$ and $m \le 2 \cdot 10^5$.
Since we need the lexicographically smallest triplet $(a, b, c)$, we should iterate through possible values of $a$ starting from 1 up to $n$. Finally, for fixed $a$ and $b$, we look for a $c > b$ such that edges $(a, b)$, $(b, c)$, and $(a, c)$ exist.                 # (shifted & -shifted) isolates the lowest set bit
                low_bit = shifted & -shifted
                # bit_length() of 2^k is k+1, so index is k = bit_length() - 1
                # The actual vertex index c is b + 1 + k
                c = (b + 1) + low_bit.bit_length() - 1
                
                print(f"{a} {b} {c}")
                return

    solve()
