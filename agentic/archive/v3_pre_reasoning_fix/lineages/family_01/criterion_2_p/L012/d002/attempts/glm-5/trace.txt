Now, consider a case where the graph has a component that is a cycle of length 4: 3-1-2-4-3. Our algorithm: i=1: 1=1, BFS: 3=2, 2=2, 4=1. Sequence: 1,2,2,1? Wait, indices: 1,2,3,4. Colors: 1=1, 2=2, 3=2, 4=1. Sequence: 1,2,2,1. Other: 2,1,1,2. 1,2,2,1 smaller. Good.

Now, consider a case where the graph has a component that is a cycle of length 4: 4-1-3-2-4. Our algorithm: i=1: 1=1, BFS: 4=2, 3=2, 2=1. Sequence: 1,1,2,2? Wait, indices: 1,2,3,4. Colors: 1=1, 2=1, 3=2, 4=2. Sequence: 1,1,2,2. Other: 2,2,1,1. 1,1,2,2 smaller. Good.

So it seems always optimal.
