To get the lexicographically smallest sequence, we should choose the assignment that gives us more 1s at the beginning. Specifically, the minimum-indexed node in each component should get gain 1.

In my BFS, I iterate through nodes 1 to n. For each unvisited node, I start a BFS and assign color 1 to it. Since I iterate from 1 to n, the first unvisited node in each component is the minimum-indexed node in that component. By assigning color 1 to this node, I ensure that it gets gain 1.
