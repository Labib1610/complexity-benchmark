# Problem B — Day and Night

Time limit: 1 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

A manufacturing plant is scheduling $n$ staff members into two distinct operational shifts, denoted as shift 1 and shift 2. 

There are $m$ tool-sharing dependencies. Each dependency involves two specific staff members who require the same specialized equipment. To prevent resource conflicts, any two staff members linked by a dependency must be placed in different shifts.

You must assign every staff member a shift number ($s_i \in \{1, 2\}$) such that no dependency is violated. If no such configuration can be formed, you must indicate this. 

When multiple valid schedules exist, the plant manager resolves the ambiguity using a sequential priority system based on staff IDs. Starting with staff member 1, the manager filters all valid schedules to only keep those where member 1 is assigned to shift 1. (If no valid schedule has member 1 in shift 1, they keep the schedules where member 1 is in shift 2). Then, from that remaining pool of schedules, the manager filters for those where staff member 2 is in shift 1, if possible. This sequential filtering continues for member 3, member 4, and so on up to $n$, until a single schedule is isolated.

## Input
The first line provides $n$ and $m$ ($1 \le n \le 2 \cdot 10^5$, $0 \le m \le 2 \cdot 10^5$).
The following $m$ lines each detail a dependency by providing two integers $u$ and $v$ — the IDs of the staff members who share a tool.

## Output
If it is impossible to schedule the staff without conflicts, print `NO` on a single line.
Otherwise, print `YES` on the first line. On the second line, print $n$ integers separated by spaces, where the $i$-th integer is the shift (1 or 2) assigned to staff member $i$, following the manager's priority system.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 4
1 2
2 3
3 4
1 4
```
Output:
```
YES
1 2 1 2
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
3 3
1 2
1 3
2 3
```
Output:
```
NO
```
