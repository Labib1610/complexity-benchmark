The affordance cannot be re-presented as suggested. Changing the undirected bridges to strictly directed edges (where a line `u v w` permits translation only from `u` to `v`) would fundamentally alter the reachability in the network. For instance, in Example 1, the inputs `1 4 1`, `2 4 1`, and `3 4 1` would make language 4 a sink, meaning the delegated languages 1, 2, and 3 could no longer be connected through it. This would change the correct output for the example and violate the hard constraint that all answers for every possible input must remain exactly the same as the seed.
