<STATEMENT>
# Problem A — Silo Synchronization

Time limit: 5 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

You are deploying a network of $n$ storage silos (indexed 1 through $n$). You have the option to establish bidirectional synchronization channels between certain pairs of silos. Establishing a channel between two silos requires a specific expenditure and permanently locks their internal registries, guaranteeing they mirror each other perfectly. This locking mechanism is transitive: any chain of channels between two silos guarantees their registries remain perfectly mirrored.

The operation depends on $t$ critical silos. Your objective is to choose a collection of channels to establish so that all $t$ critical silos are guaranteed to mirror the identical registry state. You can establish any combination of the provided channels.

Determine the minimum total expenditure of the established channels required to satisfy this condition.

## Input

The first line provides three integers $n$, $m$, and $t$. The facility's power constraints limit the total number of silos $n$ to at most the square of ten (with at least one active). The system is designed with a hardware cap on the number of critical silos $t$, which is at least one and at most the base of the standard decimal numeral system. The number of available channels $m$ is non-negative and bounded by the total number of unique two-silo combinations.

The second line provides $t$ distinct integers: the indices of the critical silos.

Following this are $m$ lines, each providing three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$. These indicate that a channel can be established between silo $u$ and silo $v$ (where $u \neq v$) for an expenditure of $w$. The expenditure $w$ is at least one and capped by a budget limit of the cube of one hundred.

It is assured that establishing all available channels would successfully mirror all critical silos.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total expenditure to ensure all critical silos share the same registry state.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 6 3
1 2 3
1 4 1
2 4 1
3 4 1
1 2 2
2 3 2
1 3 2
```

**Output**
```
3
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
4 4 3
1 2 3
1 2 1
2 3 1
1 4 10
3 4 10
```

**Output**
```
2
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 1
1
1 2 5
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
0
```

## Note

In Example 1, the condition can be met by establishing channels between (1,4), (2,4), and (3,4) for a total expenditure of 1+1+1 = 3. This forces silos 1, 2, and 3 to mirror the same state (along with silo 4). Alternatively, linking (1,2) and (2,3) requires an expenditure of 4. The minimum expenditure is 3. In Example 3, there is only one critical silo, so no channels need to be established.
