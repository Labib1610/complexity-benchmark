# Universal Translation

Time limit: 5 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A galactic federation maintains a registry of $n$ recognized languages (numbered 1 to $n$). You are organizing an interstellar summit and must distribute a critical unified broadcast to populations that speak a specific subset of $t$ priority languages.

You will draft the original broadcast in exactly one language. You may choose any of the $n$ recognized languages to be this original source.

To propagate the broadcast, there are $m$ freelance bilingual translators available for hire. Each translator is proficient in two specific languages and charges a one-time retainer fee. Once hired, a translator establishes a permanent, two-way communication link between their two languages.

The broadcast propagates automatically across any network of hired translators. Specifically, if the broadcast is available in language $X$, and you have hired a translator for languages $X$ and $Y$, the broadcast immediately becomes available in language $Y$. This propagation continues indefinitely through all connected languages. If the original source language is itself a priority language, it is available to that population immediately.

Your objective is to select an original source language and hire a set of translators such that the broadcast successfully propagates to all $t$ priority languages. Determine the minimum total retainer fee required to achieve this.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $n$, $m$, and $t$. The number of recognized languages $n$ is at least 1 and capped by the registry's maximum capacity of 100. The number of available translators $m$ is at least 0 and at most the maximum number of distinct language pairs $n(n-1)/2$. The number of priority languages $t$ is at least 1 and cannot exceed the broadcast hardware's limit of 10 priority channels.
The second line contains $t$ distinct integers: the IDs of the priority languages.
Each of the next $m$ lines contains three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$. This represents a translator for distinct languages $u$ and $v$ (both between 1 and $n$) who charges a fee $w$. The fee is at least 1 and capped by the banking regulation limit of 1000000.
The given translator options are enough to connect all priority languages (possibly through non-priority languages).

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total retainer fee required to ensure the broadcast propagates to all priority languages.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 6 3
1 2 3
1 4 1
2 4 1
3 4 1
1 2 2
2 3 2
1 3 2
```

**Output**
```
3
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
4 4 3
1 2 3
1 2 1
2 3 1
1 4 10
3 4 10
```

**Output**
```
2
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 1
1
1 2 5
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
0
```

## Note

In Example 1, you can choose language 4 as the original source. By hiring translators for 1-4, 2-4, and 3-4 (at a total cost of 1+1+1 = 3), the broadcast propagates from language 4 to the priority languages 1, 2, and 3. This is cheaper than hiring translators to directly connect the priority languages (which would cost 4). 
In Example 3, there is only one priority language. You can choose it as the original source, meaning the broadcast is available to that population immediately without requiring any translators to be hired.
