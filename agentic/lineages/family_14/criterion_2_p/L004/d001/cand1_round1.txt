# Phase 3: Synchronization Energy

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

The facility's fail-safe procedure executes in four consecutive stages:

1. **Stage 1 (Initialization)**: The mainframe activates **n** processing units (indexed 1 through n) alongside a grid of **m** two-way energy conduits. The grid is constructed so that a communication route exists between any two processing units.
2. **Stage 2 (Audit)**: An internal audit isolates exactly **t** specific units that have fallen out of sync.
3. **Stage 3 (Reconciliation - Your Task)**: The mainframe must channel power into a selection of the energy conduits. The chosen conduits must establish a fully linked grid that encompasses all **t** out-of-sync units (the grid is allowed to pass through in-sync units as intermediaries). Activating a conduit incurs a set energy cost. The mainframe needs to determine the lowest possible total energy cost to achieve this linkage.
4. **Stage 4 (Flush)**: The linked units carry out a localized data flush.

You are tasked with handling Stage 3. Based on the grid layout and the audit results from Stage 2, compute the lowest total energy cost needed to finish Stage 3.

## Input

The first line provides three integers **n**, **m**, and **t**. The number of units **n** is between 1 and 200,000, the audit count **t** is between 1 and **n**, and the conduit count **m** is strictly equal to **n** − 1.
The second line lists **t** distinct integers: the indices of the out-of-sync units.
The subsequent **m** lines each present three integers **u**, **v**, and **w** (1 ≤ u, v ≤ n, u ≠ v, 1 ≤ w ≤ 1000000), indicating a conduit between unit **u** and unit **v** that costs **w** energy to activate. The conduits comprise the interconnected grid from Stage 1.

## Output

Output a single integer representing the lowest total energy cost to link all out-of-sync units.
