<STATEMENT>
# Isotope Cascade

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

We are studying a closed system of **n** distinct isotopes, numbered 1 to **n**. There are **m** known reversible transmutation processes, each allowing direct conversion between a specific pair of isotopes. The system is fully traversable: any isotope can eventually be converted into any other through some sequence of processes. However, the system is completely devoid of redundancy—removing even a single process would permanently isolate some isotopes from others.

A recent scan has detected **t** specific isotopes in a sample. We hypothesize that they all originated from a single localized reaction event. To prove this, you must activate a subset of the available transmutation processes to form a single unified framework that connects all **t** detected isotopes together. This means that for any two detected isotopes, there must be a sequence of activated processes linking them. (If **t** = 1, no processes need to be activated). This framework is permitted to route through undetected isotopes if necessary.

Activating a transmutation process requires a certain amount of thermal energy, and the total energy is the sum of the energy requirements for all activated processes. Determine the minimum total thermal energy required to activate a subset of processes that successfully links all **t** detected isotopes.

## Input

The first line contains three integers **n**, **m**, and **t** (1 ≤ t ≤ n, 1 ≤ n ≤ 200000, m = n − 1).
The second line contains **t** distinct integers: the identifiers of the detected isotopes.
Each of the next **m** lines contains three integers **u**, **v**, and **w** (1 ≤ u, v ≤ n, u ≠ v, 1 ≤ w ≤ 1000000): a reversible transmutation process between isotope u and isotope v requiring w units of thermal energy to activate.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total thermal energy required to connect all detected isotopes.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
5 4 1
3
1 2 4
2 3 6
3 4 2
4 5 9
```

**Output**
```
0
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 3
1 2 3
1 2 4
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
9
```
