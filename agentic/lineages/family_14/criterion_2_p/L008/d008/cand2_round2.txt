<STATEMENT>
# Statutory Framework

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A comprehensive legal code consists of $R$ statutes. Over time, lawmakers have passed $S$ reciprocal cross-references between specific pairs of statutes. These cross-references weave all $R$ statutes together into a single, unified body of law.

A recent supreme court ruling mandates that $P$ specific statutes, known as the "core rights", must be extracted to form a standalone constitutional framework.

You must select a subset of the $R$ statutes to form this framework. The chosen subset must satisfy the following requirements:
1. It must include all $P$ of the core rights (you may also include any number of supplementary statutes in the subset).
2. The selected statutes must form a contiguous block of jurisprudence. That is, for any two statutes in the chosen subset, there must exist a chain of reciprocal cross-references connecting them such that every statute along the chain is also part of the chosen subset.

Whenever two statutes that share a reciprocal cross-reference are both selected for the framework, this creates an internal dependency within the new framework that must be legally reconciled. Reconciling the cross-reference between these two statutes requires a specific number of billable hours of legal work. Cross-references where at least one statute is not included in the framework point outward, are deemed external, and require no reconciliation work.

Your objective is to determine the minimum possible total billable hours required to reconcile all internal cross-references within a valid constitutional framework.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $R$, $S$, and $P$. 
The total number of statutes $R$ satisfies $1 \le R \le 200000$. 
The number of cross-references $S$ is exactly equal to $R - 1$. 
The number of core rights $P$ satisfies $1 \le P \le R$.

The second line contains $P$ distinct integers representing the identifiers of the core rights.

Each of the following $S$ lines contains three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$ ($1 \le u, v \le R$, $u \neq v$). This indicates a reciprocal cross-reference between statute $u$ and statute $v$ that requires $w$ billable hours to reconcile ($1 \le w \le 1000000$). 

## Output

Output a single integer representing the minimum total billable hours required to reconcile the internal cross-references for a valid constitutional framework.
