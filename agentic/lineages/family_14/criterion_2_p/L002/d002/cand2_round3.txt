# Orbital Station Power Grid

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A deep-space orbital facility is constructed from **n** distinct habitation and utility modules, labeled from 1 to **n**. A network of **m** pressurized transit tubes links these modules together. The facility's layout ensures that there is at least one continuous route of transit tubes between any given pair of modules. 

A critical reactor failure has forced the facility into a severe energy-rationing protocol. Most operations must be suspended, but a specific group of **t** modules houses indispensable life-support infrastructure. The facility's AI must designate a specific collection of transit tubes to remain fully powered and pressurized, ensuring that personnel can still travel safely between all **t** indispensable modules. This means that for any two indispensable modules, there must be a valid sequence of powered transit tubes connecting them, possibly passing through other modules along the way.

Every transit tube has an associated energy cost to keep it pressurized. Tubes that are not allocated power will depressurize and become impassable. Your task is to calculate the absolute minimum total energy cost required to power a collection of transit tubes that maintains full connectivity among all **t** indispensable modules.

## Input

The first line provides three integers **n**, **m**, and **t** (1 ≤ **t** ≤ **n**, 1 ≤ **n** ≤ 200000, **m** = **n** − 1).
The second line lists **t** distinct integers representing the labels of the indispensable modules.
The subsequent **m** lines each contain three integers **u**, **v**, and **w** (1 ≤ **u**, **v** ≤ **n**, **u** ≠ **v**, 1 ≤ **w** ≤ 1000000). These values indicate a transit tube connecting module **u** and module **v** which demands **w** units of energy to remain pressurized.

## Output

Output a single integer representing the minimum total energy cost to keep the indispensable modules connected.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

```

```

**Output**
```
9
```
