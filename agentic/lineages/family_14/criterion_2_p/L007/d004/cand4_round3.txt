# Interstate Commerce

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A trade federation consists of **n** planetary markets, indexed from 1 to **n**. The federation has negotiated exactly **n − 1** bilateral trade agreements. The structure of these agreements guarantees that, if all were ratified, there would be exactly one unique trade route between any given pair of planetary markets. Ratifying each agreement incurs a specific diplomatic cost.

A merchant guild has established outposts in a specific group of **t** planetary markets. Your objective is to ratify a selection of the trade agreements so that every market with a guild outpost can conduct trade with every other market with a guild outpost. Trade can be routed through planetary markets that do not have guild outposts.

The total ratification expense for your trade network is calculated by summing two components:
1. The individual diplomatic costs of all ratified trade agreements.
2. A transit tax for every planetary market without a guild outpost that is included in the active trade routes.

Currently, the transit tax is fixed at 0 per market without a guild outpost. Calculate the minimum total ratification expense necessary to link all the guild's planetary markets together, ensuring you incorporate both the diplomatic costs and the transit taxes into your final calculation.

## Input

The first line provides three integers **n**, **m**, and **t** (1 ≤ t ≤ n, 1 ≤ n ≤ 200000, m = n − 1).
The second line provides **t** distinct integers, which are the indices of the planetary markets with guild outposts.
The next **m** lines each contain three integers **u**, **v**, and **w** (1 ≤ u, v ≤ n, u ≠ v, 1 ≤ w ≤ 1000000). This indicates a bilateral trade agreement between market **u** and market **v** that costs **w** to ratify. The given network of **m** agreements guarantees that exactly one valid trade route exists between any two planetary markets when fully ratified.

## Output

Output a single integer: the minimum total ratification expense to ratify the required agreements and include the necessary markets.
