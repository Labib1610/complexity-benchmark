# Problem B — Priority Infrastructure

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A regional water authority plans to modernize the distribution network across **n** pumping stations, which are numbered consecutively starting from 1. Engineers have surveyed **m** potential pipeline segments. Each surveyed segment connects two specific stations and demands a known financial outlay to construct. The survey guarantees that building every proposed segment would create a fully integrated network where water could route between any two stations.

Due to limited funds, the authority cannot build all surveyed segments. However, they must ensure that a specific group of **t** critical stations are fully integrated. You must choose a subset of the potential segments to build such that water can flow between any two critical stations. The flow may route through intermediate stations that are not deemed critical. Determine the minimum possible total financial outlay required to achieve this integration.

## Input

The first line contains three integers **n**, **m**, and **t**. The network size **n** ranges from a minimum of 1 station to a maximum capacity of 200000 stations. The critical count **t** requires at least 1 station and cannot exceed **n**. The number of surveyed segments **m** is exactly **n** - 1.

The second line contains **t** distinct integers representing the identifiers of the critical stations.

Each of the following **m** lines contains three integers **u**, **v**, and **w**. These denote a potential pipeline segment between station **u** and station **v**, which are distinct station identifiers ranging from 1 to **n**. The financial outlay **w** requires a minimum of 1 and is capped by a maximum grant of 1000000. It is guaranteed that the full set of **m** segments would connect all **n** stations.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total financial outlay required to ensure all critical stations are connected to one another.
