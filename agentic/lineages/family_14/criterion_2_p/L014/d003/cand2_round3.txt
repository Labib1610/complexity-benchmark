<STATEMENT>
# Connecting the Critical Facilities

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A large industrial complex consists of $n$ facilities, numbered 1 to $n$. A specific subset of $t$ facilities are designated as critical operations centers. There are $m$ potential communication links that can be constructed between certain pairs of facilities, each requiring a specific construction cost.

Your objective is to select a subset of these links to build so that all $t$ critical facilities can communicate with one another, either directly or by routing through other intermediate facilities. You must achieve this requirement at the minimum possible total construction cost.

The complex's blueprint guarantees that the set of all $m$ proposed links has no redundant connections and leaves no isolated facilities. Specifically, if every proposed link were to be built, there would be exactly one sequence of links connecting any given pair of facilities in the complex.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $n$, $m$, and $t$. The total number of facilities $n$ is between 1 and the site's maximum capacity of 200000. The number of critical centers $t$ is at least 1 and at most $n$.
The second line contains $t$ distinct integers: the identifiers of the critical facilities.
Each of the next $m$ lines contains three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$. The facility identifiers $u$ and $v$ are between 1 and $n$, and $u \neq v$. These represent a potential link between facility $u$ and facility $v$ with a construction cost of $w$. The cost $w$ is at least 1 and does not exceed the project's per-link budget cap of 1000000.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total cost to ensure all critical facilities are interconnected.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
5 4 1
3
1 2 4
2 3 6
3 4 2
4 5 9
```

**Output**
```
0
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 3
1 2 3
1 2 4
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
9
```

## Note

In Example 1, the critical facilities 1 and 4 are integrated by building the links that join 1 to 2, 2 to 3, and 3 to 4, for a total cost of 5 + 1 + 2 = 8. In Example 2, there is only one critical facility, so no links need to be constructed.
</STATEMENT>
<RATIONALE>
I removed the explicit mention of `m = n - 1` and the term "single unified network", replacing them with the guarantee that building all links creates "exactly one sequence of links connecting any given pair of facilities". This forces the model to deduce the tree structure from the unique-path property rather than relying on surface-level pattern matching of the edge count. The numeric bounds have been integrated into the scenario text as capacities and budgets, preserving the exact numbers without commas to satisfy the strict I/O contract checks.
</RATIONALE>

The required output remains the minimum cost to interconnect the critical facilities, and the graph structure and weights are unchanged.
</PRESERVED_ANSWER>
