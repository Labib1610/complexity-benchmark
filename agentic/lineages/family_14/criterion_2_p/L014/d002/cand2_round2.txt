<STATEMENT>
# Activating the Critical Terminals

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A sprawling underground facility consists of **n** chambers (numbered 1 to **n**). Certain pairs of chambers can be linked by opening a sealed corridor at a specific energy cost. Among these, a subset of **t** chambers are designated as **critical**. Your objective is to select a subset of the available corridors to open so that all **t** critical chambers are linked to one another, either directly or via intermediate non-critical chambers.

To respect the facility's maximum structural capacity, the total number of chambers never exceeds 200000. Additionally, the energy cost of opening any single corridor is limited by a generator output cap of 1000000. 

You must determine the minimum total energy cost required to establish this linkage for the critical chambers. The available corridors are designed such that they can integrate the entire facility into a single contiguous complex.

## Input

The first line contains three integers **n**, **m**, and **t** (1 ≤ t ≤ n, 1 ≤ n ≤ 200000, m = n − 1).
The second line contains **t** distinct integers: the identifiers of the critical chambers.
Each of the next **m** lines contains three integers **u**, **v**, and **w** (1 ≤ u, v ≤ n, u ≠ v, 1 ≤ w ≤ 1000000), representing a corridor option linking chamber **u** and chamber **v** at an energy cost of **w**. The corridors follow the integration guarantees described above.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total energy cost to link all critical chambers.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
5 4 1
3
1 2 4
2 3 6
3 4 2
4 5 9
```

**Output**
```
0
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 3
1 2 3
1 2 4
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
9
```

## Note

In Example 1, the critical chambers 1 and 4 are linked by selecting the corridors that join 1 to 2, 2 to 3, and 3 to 4, for a total energy cost of 5 + 1 + 2 = 8. In Example 2, there is only one critical chamber, so no corridors need to be opened.

<RATIONALE>
The problem was re-themed from a networking/cabling scenario to an underground facility scenario. The explicit wording "inherently sufficient to connect all n sites together" was replaced with "integrate the entire facility into a single contiguous complex" to obscure the direct hint at a spanning tree, forcing models to infer the tree structure from the explicit `m = n - 1` and contiguous guarantee. Narrative bounds were also added to the description to contextualize the numeric limits.
</RATIONALE>
