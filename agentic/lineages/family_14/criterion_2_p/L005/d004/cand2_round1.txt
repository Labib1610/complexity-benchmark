# Fluidic Cascades

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A microfluidic chip contains **n** reaction chambers, indexed from 1 to **n**. An assay requires a specific subset of **t** essential chambers to be able to mix their contents.

The chip features **m** bidirectional microchannels, each connecting a pair of chambers. Activating a microchannel requires applying a specific thermal energy. The microchannels collectively link all chambers on the chip into a single network. Furthermore, they are arranged such that if any single microchannel were removed, its two endpoint chambers would be completely disconnected from each other, with no alternative routes between them available anywhere on the chip.

Your task is to calculate the minimum total thermal energy required to activate a subset of microchannels so that all **t** essential chambers are mutually accessible (meaning fluid can flow between any two essential chambers via a sequence of activated microchannels, potentially passing through other chambers along the way).

## Input

The first line contains three integers **n**, **m**, and **t**. The total number of chambers **n** is at least 1 and is restricted by the chip's maximum capacity of 200000. The number of microchannels **m** is exactly **n** - 1. The number of essential chambers **t** is at least 1 and at most **n**.

The second line contains **t** distinct integers: the indices of the essential chambers.

The following **m** lines each contain three integers **u**, **v**, and **w**. These specify a microchannel between chamber **u** and chamber **v** (distinct integers from 1 to **n**) requiring a thermal energy of **w** (an integer from 1 to the maximum heater output of 1000000).

## Output

Print a single integer representing the minimum total thermal energy required to ensure all essential chambers are mutually accessible.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
5 4 1
3
1 2 4
2 3 6
3 4 2
4 5 9
```

**Output**
```
0
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 3
1 2 3
1 2 4
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
9
```

## Note

In Example 1, the essential chambers 1 and 4 can only mix their contents by routing through chambers 2 and 3, meaning all three microchannels (5+1+2 = 8) must be activated. In Example 2, there is only one essential chamber, so no microchannels need to be activated.
