<STATEMENT>
# Mutagenic Pathway Synthesis

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

You are conducting a biological study on $n$ distinct protein strains, numbered $1$ to $n$. 

There are $m$ known reversible mutation pathways. Pathway $i$ allows strain $u$ to mutate into strain $v$ (and vice versa). Activating this pathway in your lab requires a one-time synthesis cost of $w$ units of catalyst. Once a pathway is activated, strains can mutate back and forth across it indefinitely without any additional catalyst.

The biological constraints guarantee that if you were to activate all $m$ pathways, any strain could eventually mutate into any other. To ensure minimal redundancy in the genome, the total number of available pathways $m$ is strictly fixed at exactly one less than $n$.

You have identified a subset of $t$ critical strains that exhibit a specific anomaly. You must activate a subset of pathways so that a single precursor strain (which you may choose arbitrarily from the $n$ available) has the ability to eventually mutate into EVERY ONE of the $t$ critical strains. A mutation from the precursor to a critical strain is possible if there is a valid sequence of activated pathways linking them.

What is the minimum total catalyst cost required to achieve this?

## Input

The first line provides three integers: $n$, $m$, and $t$. The maximum number of strains $n$ corresponds exactly to the number of days in $547$ standard $365$-day years plus $345$ days. The number of pathways $m$ is always exactly $n - 1$. The number of critical strains $t$ ranges from $1$ up to the total number of strains $n$ ($1 \le t \le n$).

The second line contains $t$ distinct integers representing the IDs of the critical strains.

Following this, there are $m$ lines, each detailing a single pathway with three integers: $u$, $v$, and $w$ ($1 \le u, v \le n$, $u \neq v$). The catalyst cost $w$ for any single pathway is at least one and does not exceed the number of millimeters in exactly one kilometer.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total catalyst cost required.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
5 4 1
3
1 2 4
2 3 6
3 4 2
4 5 9
```

**Output**
```
0
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 3
1 2 3
1 2 4
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
9
```

## Note

In Example 1, the critical strains are 1 and 4. You could choose strain 2 as the precursor. To allow strain 2 to mutate into strain 1, activate the pathway between 1 and 2 (cost 5). To allow it to mutate into strain 4, activate the pathways between 2 and 3, and 3 and 4 (costs 1 and 2). Strain 2 can now mutate into strain 1, and also mutate through strain 3 into strain 4. The total catalyst cost is 5 + 1 + 2 = 8. Alternatively, choosing strain 3 as the precursor requires exactly the same pathways. The minimum possible cost is 8.

In Example 2, there is only one critical strain (strain 3). You can simply choose strain 3 as the precursor. Since the precursor is already the critical strain, no mutations are necessary, and no pathways need to be activated. The minimum possible cost is 0.
