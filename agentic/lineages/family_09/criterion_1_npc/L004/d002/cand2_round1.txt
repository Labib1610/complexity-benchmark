The affordance cannot be re-presented. The suggested removal asks to replace "Any valid assignment meeting the criteria is accepted" with a requirement to output the lexicographically smallest set. However, the original problem explicitly accepts any valid assignment. Adding a lexicographical requirement would introduce a new tie-break rule, changing the I/O contract and restricting the set of accepted answers. This directly violates Hard Constraints 1 (Identical Answers) and 2 (Identical I/O Contract).
