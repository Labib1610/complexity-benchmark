We have an n×n matrix A, initially identity. Then m symmetric updates add 1 to A_{u,v} and A_{v,u} for pairs (u,v). Since pairs are distinct, each off-diagonal entry A_{u,v} is either 0 or 1 (well, A_{u,v} = A_{v,u} = 1 if the pair was updated, else 0). Diagonal entries are all 1.

We need to find a binary vector x with exactly k ones such that x^T A x = k.

Let's compute x^T A x. The diagonal contributes sum of x_i^2 = sum of x_i = k (since x_i ∈ {0,1}). The off-diagonal part: x^T A x = sum_{i,j} x_i A_{i,j} x_j = sum_i x_i^2 A_{i,i} + sum_{i≠j} x_i A_{i,j} x_j.

Since A is symmetric, sum_{i≠j} x_i A_{i,j} x_j = 2 * sum_{i<j} x_i A_{i,j} x_j.
