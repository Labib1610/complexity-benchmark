# Problem B — Void Channeling

Time limit: 1 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A coven of warlocks is preparing $j$ magical rituals. Each ritual possesses two intrinsic properties: an inscription duration $p_1$ and a channeling duration $p_2$.

Before beginning, the coven must arrange all $j$ rituals into a single, strict sequence from first to last.

Once the sequence is established, the ritual chain is subjected to a phenomenon known as "void resonance". A resonance surge travels through the sequence according to the following mechanics:
- The surge begins at the inscription phase of the very first ritual in the sequence.
- It moves consecutively through the inscription phases of the rituals in the established order.
- At exactly one ritual $k$ (where $1 \le k \le j$), the surge shifts from the inscription phase to the channeling phase.
- After shifting at ritual $k$, the surge then moves consecutively through the channeling phases of ritual $k$ and all subsequent rituals in the sequence, until the end.

The total intensity of a resonance surge that shifts at the $k$-th ritual is the sum of the inscription durations of the first $k$ rituals in the sequence, plus the sum of the channeling durations of the rituals from the $k$-th to the $j$-th (the last) in the sequence.

Because the surge can spontaneously shift at any of the $j$ rituals, the sequence will experience a "peak resonance", which is defined as the maximum possible intensity among all $j$ potential surge paths.

Your objective is to determine the optimal sequence in which to arrange the rituals to minimize this peak resonance. Report the smallest possible peak resonance that can be achieved.

## Input

The first line contains a single integer $j$ ($1 \le j \le 200000$).
Each of the next $j$ lines contains two integers $p_1$ and $p_2$ ($1 \le p_1, p_2 \le 1000000$): the inscription duration and the channeling duration for that ritual, respectively.

## Output

Print a single integer: the smallest possible peak resonance.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3
3 2
2 4
1 3
```

**Output**
```
10
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
1
5 3
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3
2 2
2 2
2 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

## Note

In Example 1, if the given rituals (1-indexed) are ordered 3, then 2, then 1:
The sequence of rituals has the following durations:
- 1st in sequence (Ritual 3): inscription 1, channeling 3
- 2nd in sequence (Ritual 2): inscription 2, channeling 4
- 3rd in sequence (Ritual 1): inscription 3, channeling 2

There are 3 possible paths for the resonance surge:
- Shifting at the 1st ritual: Inscription of the 1st ritual (1) + Channeling of the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd rituals (3 + 4 + 2) = 1 + 9 = 10.
- Shifting at the 2nd ritual: Inscription of the 1st and 2nd rituals (1 + 2) + Channeling of the 2nd and 3rd rituals (4 + 2) = 3 + 6 = 9.
- Shifting at the 3rd ritual: Inscription of the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd rituals (1 + 2 + 3) + Channeling of the 3rd ritual (2) = 6 + 2 = 8.

The peak resonance for this sequence is $\max(10, 9, 8) = 10$. No other sequence results in a smaller peak resonance.
