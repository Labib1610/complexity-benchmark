<STATEMENT>
# Pilgrim's Burden

Time limit: 1 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A series of **j** ancient ritual pillars are to be erected in a line from west to east. Each pillar features two sacred inscriptions: a `sun` inscription and a `moon` inscription. Each inscription has a numeric resonance value.

A pilgrim begins their journey at the `sun` inscription of the westernmost pillar and travels east. At exactly one pillar along the journey, the pilgrim will transition from the `sun` inscriptions to the `moon` inscriptions. Once they transition to the `moon` inscriptions, they must remain on `moon` inscriptions for the remainder of their journey through the easternmost pillar.

The pilgrim's total accumulated burden is the sum of the resonance values of all inscriptions they visit. Specifically, if they transition at the **k**-th pillar (where 1 ≤ k ≤ j), their burden is the sum of the `sun` resonance values from the 1st pillar up to and including the **k**-th pillar, plus the sum of the `moon` resonance values from the **k**-th pillar up to and including the **j**-th pillar.

The pilgrim possesses absolute foresight and will always choose the transition point **k** that maximizes their total burden.

You must determine the initial west-to-east ordering of all **j** pillars that minimizes the maximum burden the pilgrim can achieve. Report this minimized maximum burden.

## Input

The first line contains a single integer **j** (1 ≤ j ≤ 200000).
Each of the next **j** lines contains two integers **p1** and **p2** (1 ≤ p1, p2 ≤ 1000000): the resonance value of the pillar's `sun` inscription, and the resonance value of its `moon` inscription, respectively.

## Output

Print a single integer: the smallest possible maximum burden the pilgrim can accumulate, given the optimal arrangement of the pillars.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3
3 2
2 4
1 3
```

**Output**
```
10
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
1
5 3
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3
2 2
2 2
2 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

## Note

In Example 1, if the pillars are ordered such that their (sun, moon) values are (1, 3), (2, 4), and (3, 2) from west to east:
- Transitioning at the 1st pillar gives a burden of 1 (sun) + 3 + 4 + 2 (moons) = 10.
- Transitioning at the 2nd pillar gives a burden of 1 + 2 (suns) + 4 + 2 (moons) = 9.
- Transitioning at the 3rd pillar gives a burden of 1 + 2 + 3 (suns) + 2 (moons) = 8.

The pilgrim will choose to transition at the 1st pillar, resulting in a maximum burden of 10. No other arrangement of the 3 pillars can yield a maximum burden strictly less than 10.
