<STATEMENT>
# Problem B — Protein Synthesis

Time limit: 1 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A batch of **j** protein samples must be synthesized. You must define a single, strict sequential processing queue, determining exactly which sample is processed first, second, and so on.

The laboratory utilizes two specialized biological agents: the Cleaving Enzyme and the Folding Enzyme. Each sample must first be catalyzed by the Cleaving Enzyme (taking **t1** minutes), and subsequently stabilized by the Folding Enzyme (taking **t2** minutes).

The laboratory protocols enforce these strict rules:
- The Cleaving Enzyme processes samples in the exact sequence of your queue. It begins with the first sample at minute 0 and works continuously, moving to the next sample the exact instant the previous one is fully cleaved.
- The Folding Enzyme also processes samples in the exact sequence of your queue.
- A sample begins its folding process at the earliest minute when both of the following conditions are met:
  1. The sample has been completely catalyzed by the Cleaving Enzyme.
  2. The Folding Enzyme is free (if the sample is not the first in the queue, the preceding sample in the queue must have completely finished its folding process).

Your objective is to determine the optimal queue sequence that minimizes the exact minute when the final sample in the queue completes its folding process.

## Input

The first line contains a single integer **j**. The laboratory's incubator capacity dictates that the batch size is at least one and at most exactly two hundred thousand samples.

Each of the next **j** lines contains exactly two integers **t1** and **t2**: the cleaving duration and the folding duration of a sample. Chemical stability limits dictate that each duration is at least one and at most exactly one million minutes.

## Output

Print a single integer: the smallest possible minute at which the final sample in your queue completes its folding process.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3
3 2
2 4
1 3
```

**Output**
```
10
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
1
5 3
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3
2 2
2 2
2 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

## Note

In Example 2, the single sample has a cleaving duration of 5 and a folding duration of 3. Its cleaving begins at 0 and ends at 5. Its folding begins immediately at 5, ending at minute 5 + 3 = 8.
