# Problem B — Energy Grid Balancing

Time limit: 1 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

You are managing an energy grid that must support **j** distinct industrial operations.

You must establish a single execution sequence (an ordering of the **j** operations). The grid has a central capacitor which starts with an initial charge of 0 units.

For each operation in your chosen sequence, the following two steps occur in exact order:
1. **Power Draw**: The operation requires **p1** units of energy. If the capacitor's current charge $C$ is less than **p1**, the capacitor is fully depleted to 0, and the grid must draw the shortfall of $(p1 - C)$ units from an external emergency generator. If $C \ge p1$, the capacitor's charge simply decreases by **p1** and the emergency generator is not used.
2. **Power Generation**: The operation then generates **p2** units of energy. This energy is entirely stored in the capacitor, increasing its charge by **p2**.

To evaluate the efficiency of your sequence, you calculate its **Total Energy Footprint**. This footprint is defined as the total energy drawn from the emergency generator across all **j** operations, plus the total energy generated by all **j** operations. 

Your task is to find an ordering of the **j** operations that minimizes the Total Energy Footprint.

What is the minimum possible Total Energy Footprint?

## Input

The first line contains a single integer **j** (1 ≤ j ≤ 200000).
Each of the following **j** lines contains two integers **p1** and **p2** (1 ≤ p1, p2 ≤ 1000000), representing the power draw and power generation of the operation, respectively.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum possible Total Energy Footprint.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3
3 2
2 4
1 3
```

**Output**
```
10
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
1
5 3
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3
2 2
2 2
2 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

## Note

In Example 1, if we order the operations as the 3rd operation, then the 2nd operation, then the 1st operation from the input:
The sequence of power draws is $p1 = [1, 2, 3]$ and power generations is $p2 = [3, 4, 2]$.
Tracing the capacitor's charge:
- **Operation 3**:
  - Draw: requires 1. Capacitor has 0. Emergency draws 1. Capacitor becomes 0.
  - Generation: generates 3. Capacitor becomes 3.
- **Operation 2**:
  - Draw: requires 2. Capacitor has 3. No emergency draw. Capacitor becomes 1.
  - Generation: generates 4. Capacitor becomes 5.
- **Operation 1**:
  - Draw: requires 3. Capacitor has 5. No emergency draw. Capacitor becomes 2.
  - Generation: generates 2. Capacitor becomes 4.

Total emergency energy drawn = $1 + 0 + 0 = 1$.
Total energy generated = $3 + 4 + 2 = 9$.
Total Energy Footprint = $1 + 9 = 10$.
This is the minimum possible footprint across all valid orderings.

In Example 2, there is only one operation. 
- Draw: requires 5. Capacitor has 0. Emergency draws 5. Capacitor becomes 0.
- Generation: generates 3. Capacitor becomes 3.
Total emergency energy drawn = 5. Total energy generated = 3. Footprint = $5 + 3 = 8$.
