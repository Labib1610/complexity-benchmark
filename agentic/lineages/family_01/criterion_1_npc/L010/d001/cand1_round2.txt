# Problem A — Deployment Phases

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

A heavy machinery facility employs $n$ machinists. Due to strict safety protocols, certain pairs of machinists cannot operate concurrently because their combined workflows create hazards. The facility schedules its daily operations across three distinct deployment phases labeled 1, 2, and 3. 

To prevent accidents, any two machinists who share a hazard must not be scheduled during the same deployment phase. You must allocate every machinist to exactly one of the three phases such that no two machinists with a hazard are in the same phase. If it is impossible to schedule them under these restrictions, you must report it.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ — the number of machinists and the number of documented hazards. The headcount $n$ is bounded by the minimum viable crew of $1$ and the facility's maximum locker capacity of $40$. The number of hazards $m$ can be as low as $0$ and as high as $n \cdot (n-1)/2$ (the physical limit where every distinct pair of machinists shares a hazard).

Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — a pair of machinists who share a hazard.

## Output
If no valid phase allocation can be found, print a single line containing NO.
Otherwise, print YES on the first line. On the second line, print $n$ integers $s_1, s_2, \ldots, s_n$ (each chosen from $\{1, 2, 3\}$), where $s_i$ is the deployment phase assigned to machinist $i$. Any valid allocation is accepted.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
1 4
2 4
```
Output:
```
YES
1 2 3 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
4 6
1 2
1 3
1 4
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
NO
```
