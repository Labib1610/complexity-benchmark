 We need to find a triangle (3 mutually connected nodes) in the graph, with the smallest lexicographic order of its vertices.

Since the graph is guaranteed to have at least one triangle, we can iterate over vertices in increasing order, and for each vertex, check pairs of its neighbors to see if they are connected. The first triangle found in this order will be the lexicographically smallest.

Here’s the implementation:

```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
adj = [set() for _ in range(n)]
for _ in range(m):
    u, v = map(int, input().split())
    u -= 1
    v -= 1
    adj[u].add(v)
    adj[v].add(u)

for a in range(n):
    for b in adj[a]:
        if b > a:
            for c in adj[a]:
                if c > b and c in adj[b]:
                    print(a + 1, b + 1, c + 1)
                    exit()
```
