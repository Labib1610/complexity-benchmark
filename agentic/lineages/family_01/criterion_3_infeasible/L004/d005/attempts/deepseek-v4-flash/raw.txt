 We need to find a triple of vertices whose adjacency matrix has determinant 2.  
For a 3×3 symmetric zero-diagonal matrix, the determinant is:

\[
\det(S) = 2 \cdot a_{12} a_{13} a_{23}
\]

where \(a_{ij}\) is 1 if edge exists, 0 otherwise.  
So determinant = 2 means the three vertices form a triangle (all three edges present).  

Thus the problem reduces to: find the lexicographically smallest triangle in the graph.

We can do this by iterating over vertices in increasing order, and for each pair of neighbors, check if they are connected.

Here’s the implementation:

```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
adj = [set() for _ in range(n)]
for _ in range(m):
    u, v = map(int, input().split())
    u -= 1
    v -= 1
    adj[u].add(v)
    adj[v].add(u)

for i in range(n):
    for j in adj[i]:
        if j > i:
            for k in adj[j]:
                if k > j and k in adj[i]:
                    print(i + 1, j + 1, k + 1)
                    exit()
```
