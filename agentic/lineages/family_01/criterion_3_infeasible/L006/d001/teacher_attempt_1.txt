# Problem C — Vault Impossibility Certificate

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

You are responsible for safely storing a collection of delicate historical artifacts, which are labeled sequentially starting from 1. The facility provides exactly two climate-controlled vaults. Because the artifacts are made from various materials, certain pairs react poorly to the same atmospheric settings and are forbidden from sharing a vault.

It turns out that the given list of forbidden pairs makes it impossible to partition the entire collection into just two vaults. You must provide a direct proof of this failure. To do this, find a trio of artifacts where the rules forbid the first from sharing a vault with the second, forbid the second from sharing with the third, and forbid the first from sharing with the third. By the pigeonhole principle, placing these three artifacts into two vaults will inevitably pair two of them together, violating the restrictions. Find and output one such trio.

## Input
The first line provides two integers $n$ and $m$, indicating the total artifact count and the number of forbidden combinations. Because a valid trio is guaranteed to exist, the collection contains at least 3 artifacts, and it is bounded by the facility's hard limit of two thousand items. The database of $m$ records is also guaranteed to have at least 3 entries, and its capacity is capped at two hundred thousand entries (or the total possible number of distinct pairs, if that is smaller).
The following $m$ lines each contain two integers $u$ and $v$, representing a forbidden pair.
At least one such impossible trio is guaranteed to be present.

## Output
Output three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$, separated by spaces, with $a < b < c$, representing the trio. All three pairs ($a$ and $b$, $b$ and $c$, $a$ and $c$) must be forbidden by the input list. If multiple such trios exist, output the lexicographically earliest one (prioritizing the smallest $a$, then the smallest $b$, then the smallest $c$).

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
