# Problem C — Grievance Audit

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

The factory's human resources department is auditing past worker schedules to understand why a complete operational halt occurred. You are provided with a historical archive of filed grievances. Each grievance establishes a mutual incompatibility between two distinct employees. The archive contains no duplicate records; every pair of incompatible employees appears at most once.

Your goal is to process this archive to isolate a trilateral impasse. A trilateral impasse is a group of exactly three distinct employees, which we will identify as $a$, $b$, and $c$, that satisfies all of the following conditions:
1. The archive contains a grievance record for the pair $a$ and $b$.
2. The archive contains a grievance record for the pair $a$ and $c$.
3. The archive contains a grievance record for the pair $b$ and $c$.
4. Exactly 3 grievance records in the archive have the property that neither of the two employees involved is outside the set $\{a, b, c\}$.
5. The absolute difference between the largest and smallest employee identifiers in the group is at least 2.

We know that during this time, it was strictly impossible to divide the employees into two shifts without internal grievances. By the nature of this scheduling failure, the archive is guaranteed to hold at least one valid trilateral impasse that satisfies all five conditions. You must find such a group.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$), which are the total number of employees and the total number of filed grievances, respectively. Employees are identified by integers from $1$ to $n$.
The next $m$ lines each contain two integers $u$ and $v$ ($1 \le u, v \le n$, $u \ne v$), indicating that employee $u$ and employee $v$ have a mutual grievance. The order of the two integers on a line does not matter.
It is guaranteed that there exists at least one trilateral impasse in the dataset.

## Output
Print a single line containing three space-separated integers $a$, $b$, and $c$, arranged in strictly increasing order ($a < b < c$). These three integers must be the identifiers of the employees who make up a trilateral impasse (meaning the pairs $(a,b)$, $(a,c)$, and $(b,c)$ all appear as grievances in the archive, and the group satisfies the additional structural conditions). If the archive contains multiple valid impasses, you must output the one that is lexicographically smallest (i.e., among all valid impasses, output the one with the minimum possible value for $a$; in case of a tie, minimize $b$; and if still tied, minimize $c$).

## Example 1
Input:
```

1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2

```
5 6

1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
