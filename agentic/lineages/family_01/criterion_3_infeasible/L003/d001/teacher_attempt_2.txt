# Problem C — Roster Evaluation

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

The factory management is reviewing the worker roster and a list of known grievances between pairs of employees. Your task is to process this roster and identify a specific group. 

HR requires a test committee of exactly three personnel. For the committee to be valid, if you select any two members from this committee of three, they must have a grievance recorded between them. It is guaranteed that at least one such valid committee can be formed. Output the members of one such committee.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$. The factory's headcount $n$ is at least 3, and cannot exceed the total capacity of its 40 departments, each of which holds exactly 50 personnel. The number of grievance entries $m$ is at least 3, and cannot exceed two hundred thousand, nor can it exceed the total number of possible unique pairs of employees.

Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — a pair of employees who have a grievance against each other.

It is guaranteed that at least one valid committee of three exists.

## Output
Print three integers $a$ $b$ $c$ on one line with $a < b < c$ representing the employee IDs of the chosen committee. If there are multiple valid committees, output the one that is lexicographically first: the one with the smallest $a$; in case of a tie, the smallest $b$; and if still tied, the smallest $c$.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```

</RATIONALE>
