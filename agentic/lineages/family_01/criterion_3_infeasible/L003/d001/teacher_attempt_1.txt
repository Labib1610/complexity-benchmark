# Problem C — Roster Evaluation

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

The factory management is reviewing the worker roster and a list of known bilateral interactions between pairs of workers. Your task is to process this roster and identify a specific group. We define a "triad" as a set of exactly three distinct workers where every worker in the set has a recorded interaction with both of the other two workers in the set.

Each triad $\{a, b, c\}$ (ordered such that $a < b < c$) is assigned a unique signature calculated by the formula $a \cdot 10^8 + b \cdot 10^4 + c$. Your task is to find the triad that has the strictly minimum signature among all triads present in the roster. It is guaranteed that the records contain at least one triad.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$), representing the number of workers and the number of recorded interactions.
Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — a pair of workers who have interacted.
It is guaranteed that at least one triad exists in the given records.

## Output
Print three integers $a$ $b$ $c$ on one line with $a < b < c$, representing the members of the triad with the minimum signature.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
