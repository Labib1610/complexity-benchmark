# Audit of Entangled Assets

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

A forensic accounting firm is analyzing a registry of corporate entities. The goal is to partition the entities into two distinct regulatory zones such that no direct financial exchanges exist between any two entities assigned to the same zone. The current registry cannot be partitioned in this way.

The analysts have determined that this failure is caused by a structural anomaly: a trio of entities configured such that each entity has a direct financial exchange with both of the other two. Because all three entities directly exchange assets with each other, it is impossible to assign them to just two zones without at least two exchanging entities sharing a zone.

Your task is to pinpoint such a trio. If there are multiple such trios, you must isolate the one that minimizes the anomaly fingerprint. For a trio of three entities with identification numbers $a, b,$ and $c$ (ordered so that $a < b < c$), the anomaly fingerprint is defined by the formula: $a \cdot 10^8 + b \cdot 10^4 + c$.

## Input
The first line of the input contains two integers $n$ and $m$, representing the number of corporate entities and the number of recorded financial exchanges. The entities are assigned integer IDs from $1$ to $n$. The auditing software is licensed for a maximum of two thousand corporate entities, and the database limits the transaction log to at most two hundred thousand records. There are at least three entities and three records, and the number of records will not exceed the total possible number of unique entity pairs.

Each of the following $m$ lines contains two distinct integers $u$ and $v$ corresponding to valid entity IDs, indicating that a direct financial exchange exists between entity $u$ and entity $v$. 

The provided records are guaranteed to contain at least one trio of entities where each entity is directly linked to both of the other two.

## Output
Output a single line containing three space-separated integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ ($a < b < c$) representing the IDs of the three entities in the trio. You must output the trio that yields the minimum anomaly fingerprint as described.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
