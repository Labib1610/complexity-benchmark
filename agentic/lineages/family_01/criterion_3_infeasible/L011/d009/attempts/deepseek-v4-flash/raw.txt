We need to find three vertices whose adjacency vectors (in the graph sense) are linearly independent over the reals.  

The vectors are the rows of the adjacency matrix (without self-loops). For three vertices \(a, b, c\), the vectors are:

- \(v_a\): entries for \(b\) and \(c\) (and others)  
- \(v_b\): entries for \(a\) and \(c\)  
- \(v_c\): entries for \(a\) and \(b\)  

Since we only care about the three chosen vertices, the relevant part is the 3×3 submatrix:

\[
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & e_{ab} & e_{ac} \\
e_{ab} & 0 & e_{bc} \\
e_{ac} & e_{bc} & 0
\end{bmatrix}
\]

where \(e_{xy} = 1\) if edge exists, else 0.

The determinant of this matrix is:

\[
\det = 2 \cdot e_{ab} \cdot e_{ac} \cdot e_{bc}
\]

So the vectors are linearly independent iff \(e_{ab} = e_{ac} = e_{bc} = 1\), i.e., the three vertices form a triangle.

Thus the problem reduces to finding the lexicographically smallest triangle in the graph.

We can do this by iterating over \(a\) from 1 to n, then \(b\) from \(a+1\) to n, and checking if edge \(a-b\) exists. If yes, then for each \(c > b\), check if edges \(a-c\) and \(b-c\) exist. The first found is the answer.

Complexity:  
We can store adjacency as sets for O(1) edge checks.  
Worst-case: \(O(n^3)\) if naive, but with n=2000 and m up to 2e5, we can optimize by only checking pairs that have an edge.

We can iterate over edges (u,v) with u<v, and for each such edge, look for a common neighbor w > v.  
We can do this by iterating over neighbors of u and checking if they are also neighbors of v and > v.

Implementation:

```python
import sys

def main():
    input = sys.stdin.readline
    n, m = map(int, input().split())
    adj = [set() for _ in range(n+1)]
    edges = []
    for _ in range(m):
        u, v = map(int, input().split())
        adj[u].add(v)
        adj[v].add(u)
        if u > v:
            u, v = v, u
        edges.append((u, v))
    
    # Sort edges by u then v
    edges.sort()
    
    for u, v in edges:
        # Find common neighbor w > v
        # Iterate over neighbors of u that are > v
        for w in adj[u]:
            if w > v and w in adj[v]:
                print(u, v, w)
                return

if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```
