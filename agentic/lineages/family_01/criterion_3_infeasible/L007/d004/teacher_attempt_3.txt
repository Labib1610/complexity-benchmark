# Legislative Committees

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

A parliament has $n$ elected representatives. Through various bipartisan efforts, $m$ pairs of representatives have established mutual working agreements.

To form a new "Steering Council", the parliamentary rules require finding a group of exactly three distinct representatives $a$, $b$, and $c$ (with $a < b < c$) where every pair within the group shares an active working agreement. Specifically, an agreement must exist between $a$ and $b$, between $b$ and $c$, and between $a$ and $c$.

With up to 2000 representatives, the endorsement records are extensive. It is guaranteed that at least one such group of three exists. You must identify it.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$).
Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$, indicating that representative $u$ and representative $v$ have a mutual working agreement. 
It is guaranteed that at least one valid group of three representatives exists.

## Output
Print three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on one line with $a < b < c$ that form a Steering Council. 
If multiple valid groups exist, output the one with the smallest value for $a$; if there is a tie, choose the one with the smallest value for $b$; and if still tied, the smallest value for $c$.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
