# Problem C — Volatile Complex

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

You are evaluating a mixture of $n$ artificial proteins. Through pairwise testing, you have recorded $m$ symmetric interactions where two specific proteins are capable of bonding.

A volatile "tripartite structure" occurs when a group of three distinct proteins mutually interact—meaning any two proteins within this group are capable of bonding. The mixture is known to harbor at least one tripartite structure. Your task is to determine the identities of the three proteins involved.

## Input
The first line contains $n$ and $m$ (3 ≤ n ≤ 2000, 3 ≤ m ≤ min(n·(n−1)/2, 2·10⁵)), representing the number of artificial proteins and the number of observed interactions, respectively.
The following $m$ lines each contain two integers $u$ and $v$, indicating that protein $u$ and protein $v$ interact.
The input is guaranteed to contain at least one tripartite structure.

## Output
Print three integers on a single line, separated by spaces, representing the proteins forming the tripartite structure. The three identifiers must be printed in ascending order. 

If more than one tripartite structure exists, you must select the one whose smallest protein identifier is as small as possible. If there are multiple candidates that share the same smallest identifier, resolve the tie by minimizing the middle identifier, and if still tied, the largest identifier.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
