# Problem C — Policy Steering Group

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

At a global summit, there are $n$ diplomats in attendance. Throughout the summit, $m$ memorandums of understanding (MOUs) are signed. Each MOU is a mutual agreement involving exactly two different diplomats.

According to the summit's charter, a "policy steering group" must consist of exactly three diplomats who have all signed MOUs with one another. In other words, within the chosen group of three, every possible pair among them must have an MOU in place.

You have been provided with the complete registry of all signed MOUs. It is guaranteed that the registry contains enough agreements to form at least one policy steering group. Your task is to identify the members of such a group.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$), representing the number of diplomats and the number of MOUs, respectively. Diplomats are assigned ID numbers from $1$ to $n$.

Each of the following $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ ($1 \le u, v \le n$, $u \neq v$), indicating that an MOU was signed between diplomat $u$ and diplomat $v$.

It is guaranteed that at least one policy steering group can be formed.

## Output
Print three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on a single line, separated by spaces, representing the ID numbers of the diplomats in a policy steering group. The ID numbers must be printed in strictly increasing order ($a < b < c$).

If multiple policy steering groups exist, output the one that is lexicographically smallest (i.e., minimize $a$; in case of a tie, minimize $b$; and if still tied, minimize $c$).

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
