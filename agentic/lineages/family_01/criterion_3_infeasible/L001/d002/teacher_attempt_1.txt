# Problem C — Policy Steering Group

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

At a global summit, $n$ diplomats are in attendance. Throughout the summit, $m$ memorandums of understanding (MOUs) are signed. Each MOU is a mutual agreement involving exactly two different diplomats.

A "policy steering group" is a committee consisting of three diplomats. For the committee to be valid, each diplomat in the trio must have a recorded MOU with both of their colleagues in that same trio.

You have been provided with the complete registry of all signed MOUs. The registry is guaranteed to contain the necessary agreements to form at least one policy steering group. Your task is to identify the members of such a group.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$. The summit venue provides private offices for the diplomats, meaning the headcount $n$ is at least three but cannot exceed the venue's maximum capacity of two thousand. The summit's archival system can record up to two hundred thousand MOUs. The number of signed MOUs, $m$, will be at least three, and will not exceed the archival limit or the total number of possible pairs among the attendees, whichever is more restrictive. Diplomats are assigned ID numbers sequentially from one to $n$.

Each of the following $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ ($u \neq v$), indicating that an MOU was signed between the diplomat with ID $u$ and the diplomat with ID $v$. The IDs $u$ and $v$ are bounded by the lowest possible ID (one) and the total number of attending diplomats $n$.

It is guaranteed that at least one policy steering group can be formed.

## Output
Output three space-separated integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on a single line, corresponding to the IDs of the diplomats in the identified group. These values must be arranged in strictly increasing order ($a < b < c$).

Should there be more than one valid committee, provide the one that is lexicographically smallest. This means you must minimize $a$; if there is a tie, minimize $b$; and if a tie still remains, minimize $c$.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
