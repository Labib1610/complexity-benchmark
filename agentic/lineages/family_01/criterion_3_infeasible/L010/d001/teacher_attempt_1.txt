# Problem C — Proof of Impossibility

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

The factory management wants to divide the workforce into exactly two shifts, but the roster is known to be hopeless. Your job is to provide concrete evidence that a two-shift schedule is unworkable. To do this, you must find a trio of workers who are completely deadlocked: each worker in the trio has a mutual grievance on file with both of the other two members of the trio. Such a group undeniably proves that two shifts are not enough. Finding this specific trio can appear daunting given the sheer number of possible combinations to check.

The factory employs $n$ workers, identified by integers from 1 to $n$. A minimum of three workers are required to operate, while maximum building occupancy caps the headcount at exactly two thousand. The HR department has collected $m$ mutual grievance forms, each naming two workers who refuse to be on the same shift. A minimum of three forms have been filed. The physical filing cabinet holds a maximum of two hundred thousand forms, and the number of forms will never exceed the theoretical maximum of one form per unique pair of workers.

It is guaranteed that the HR data contains at least one completely deadlocked trio. Output one such set.

## Input
The first line contains the headcount $n$ and the number of grievance forms $m$.
Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — the IDs of a pair of workers named on a single grievance form.
It is guaranteed that at least one deadlocked trio exists in the provided data.

## Output
Print three integers on one line, representing the IDs of the workers in a deadlocked trio, sorted from lowest to highest. If there are multiple valid trios, output the one that is lexicographically smallest (the one with the lowest first ID; in case of a tie, the lowest second ID; and if still tied, the lowest third ID).

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
