# Problem C — Proof of Impossibility

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

Management is attempting to divide the factory workforce into exactly two shifts, but the proposed constraints make this impossible. Your task is to provide concrete evidence of this impossibility rather than attempting to build a schedule. 

You are given a log of conflict records; each record indicates a specific pair of workers who refuse to be assigned to the same shift. The logs guarantee the existence of a minimal impossible group: three workers such that a conflict record exists for every possible pair chosen from this group. Identifying any such group conclusively demonstrates that two shifts are inadequate. You must find and output one such group.

## Input
The first line provides two integers, $n$ and $m$, representing the number of workers and the number of conflict records, respectively. The factory requires a minimum operating headcount of three personnel, and the site's maximum safety capacity is two thousand personnel, bounding $n$. The grievance system always contains at least three records, and its physical storage limit is two hundred thousand reports (though it never exceeds the total number of unique worker pairs), bounding $m$. Workers are identified by integers from $1$ to $n$.
The following $m$ lines each contain two integers $u$ and $v$ ($1 \le u, v \le n, u \neq v$), representing a conflict between worker $u$ and worker $v$.
It is guaranteed that the provided records contain at least one group of three workers where every pair within the group is listed as a conflict.

## Output
Output three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on a single line, ordered such that $a < b < c$, representing a group of three workers where the conflicts $a$–$b$, $a$–$c$, and $b$–$c$ are all present in the input. If there is more than one such group, you must output the one that minimizes the conflict score, defined as $a \cdot 10^8 + b \cdot 10^4 + c$.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
