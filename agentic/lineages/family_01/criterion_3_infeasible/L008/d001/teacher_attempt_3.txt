# Problem C — Tri-genic Interaction Complex

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

In a recent genetic study, researchers are analyzing $n$ distinct genetic markers, numbered 1 through $n$. Through extensive screening, they have identified $m$ specific pairs of markers that exhibit epistatic interference when they appear together.

The researchers are searching for a highly specific structure known as a tri-genic interaction complex. This complex consists of exactly three genetic markers where every possible pair drawn from these three markers appears in the list of interfering pairs. 

You are provided with the list of interfering pairs. It is guaranteed that the dataset contains at least one tri-genic interaction complex. Your task is to identify and output the markers forming one such complex.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$).
Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — a pair of genetic markers that exhibit epistatic interference.
It is guaranteed that at least one such complex of three markers exists.

## Output
Print three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on one line with $a < b < c$ such that all three pairs $a$-$b$, $a$-$c$, and $b$-$c$ are present in the list of interfering pairs. Output the lexicographically smallest such group (smallest $a$; ties broken by smallest $b$; then smallest $c$).

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
