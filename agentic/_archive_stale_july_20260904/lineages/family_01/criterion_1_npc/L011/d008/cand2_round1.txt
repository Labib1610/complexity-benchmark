# Problem A — Team Roster

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

You are organizing a tournament with $n$ players, numbered $1$ to $n$. You must assign each player to exactly one of 3 teams: Team 1, Team 2, or Team 3.

There are $m$ rivalries among the players. A valid tournament roster ensures that for every rivalry between player $u$ and player $v$, the two players are assigned to different teams.

When multiple valid assignments exist, they are ranked by comparing the team numbers given to the players in order of their IDs. To determine which of two valid rosters is preferred, identify the lowest player ID that receives a different team number in the two rosters. The roster that gives the smaller team number (1, 2, or 3) to this specific player is considered strictly better. Your goal is to output the single valid roster that is preferred over all other valid rosters. If it is impossible to form a valid roster, report that no solution exists.

*Performance note:* The evaluation data includes highly adversarial rivalry networks. Any approach that simply guesses team assignments sequentially without advanced logical deduction or eliminating unviable branches early will perform vastly more operations than the time limit permits before finding the correct roster or proving none exists.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$. The number of players $n$ is at least 1 and strictly less than the 13th prime number (where 2 is considered the 1st prime). The number of rivalries $m$ is a non-negative integer that will not exceed the total number of unique unordered pairs among the $n$ players.
Each of the following $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ ($1 \le u, v \le n$, $u \neq v$), indicating that player $u$ and player $v$ have a rivalry.

## Output
If no valid roster can be formed, print a single line containing `NO`.
If a valid roster is possible, print `YES` on the first line. On the second line, print $n$ integers $c_1, c_2, \ldots, c_n$, where $c_i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ is the team assigned to player $i$. If there are multiple valid rosters, you must output the one that is preferred according to the tie-breaking rules described above.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
1 4
2 4
```
Output:
```
YES
1 2 3 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
4 6
1 2
1 3
1 4
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
NO
```
