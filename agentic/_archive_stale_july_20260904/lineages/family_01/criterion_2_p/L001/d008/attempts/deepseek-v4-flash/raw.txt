 We know each \( r_i \) is either 1 or 2.  
The energy formula is:

\[
E = \sum_{i=1}^n 6 D_i r_i^2 - \sum_{j=1}^m 14 r_{u_j} r_{v_j}
\]

Since \( r_i^2 = 1 \) for both 1 and 2, the first sum is constant:  
\[
\sum 6 D_i = 6 \cdot 2m = 12m
\]
because each link contributes 2 to the sum of degrees.

So:

\[
E = 12m - 14 \sum_{j=1}^m r_{u_j} r_{v_j}
\]

We want \( E = 2m \), so:

\[
12m - 14S = 2m \quad \Rightarrow \quad 10m = 14S \quad \Rightarrow \quad S = \frac{5m}{7}
\]

Thus, \( m \) must be divisible by 7, and the sum of products over edges must be exactly \( 5m/7 \).

Now, each \( r_i \in \{1,2\} \). Let’s define \( x_i = r_i - 1 \), so \( x_i \in \{0,1\} \).  
Then:

\[
r_u r_v = (1+x_u)(1+x_v) = 1 + x_u + x_v + x_u x_v
\]

Sum over edges:

\[
S = m + \sum (x_u + x_v) + \sum x_u x_v
\]

But \(\sum (x_u + x_v) = \sum_i \deg(i) x_i = 2m \cdot \text{avg?}\) Actually, \(\sum_i \deg(i) x_i\) is just sum over edges of \(x_u + x_v\).

Let \( T = \sum_i \deg(i) x_i \) and \( P = \sum_{(u,v)} x_u x_v \). Then:

\[
S = m + T + P
\]

We need \( S = 5m/7 \), so:

\[
m + T + P = \frac{5m}{7} \quad \Rightarrow \quad T + P = -\frac{2m}{7}
\]

Since \( T \ge 0 \) and \( P \ge 0 \), this is impossible unless \( m = 0 \).  

Wait — check: if \( m=0 \), then \( S=0 \) and \( 5m/7=0 \), works. But for \( m>0 \), impossible.  

So the only solution is \( m=0 \). Then all \( r_i \) can be anything, but lexicographically smallest is all 1’s.

Thus:

- If \( m \neq 0 \), output `NO`.
- If \( m = 0 \), output `YES` and all 1’s.

```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
for _ in range(m):
    input()
if m == 0:
    print("YES")
    print(" ".join(["1"] * n))
else:
    print("NO")
```
