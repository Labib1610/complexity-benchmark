# Problem B — Day and Night

Time limit: 1 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

A manufacturing plant employs a workforce of $n$ people, identified by integers starting from 1. The plant operates exactly two daily schedules, designated as schedule 1 and schedule 2. Every person must be assigned to exactly one of these two schedules. 

Due to equipment shortages, there are $m$ specific pairs of employees who share specialized tools. Two people in such a pair cannot be assigned to the identical schedule. 

You must determine if a valid assignment of schedules to all $n$ employees exists. If there are multiple valid ways to assign the schedules, you must choose the one that is lexicographically first. That is, if you list the schedule assignments for employees 1, 2, 3, ..., $n$ in order, the resulting sequence of 1s and 2s should be as small as possible.

## Input
The first line provides two integers: the headcount $n$ and the number of tool-sharing pairs $m$. The plant operates 200 departments, each with a strict allocation of 1,000 safety badges. The headcount $n$ is at least 1 and cannot exceed the plant's total badge capacity. The number of pairs $m$ is at least 0 and is also bounded by this same total capacity.
The subsequent $m$ lines each contain two integers, representing a pair of employees who must be given different schedules.

## Output
If it is impossible to assign schedules without violating the tool-sharing constraints, print a single line containing `NO`.
Otherwise, print `YES` on the first line. On the second line, print $n$ integers (each either 1 or 2) representing the lexicographically first valid schedule assignment for employees 1 through $n$.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 4
1 2
2 3
3 4
1 4
```
Output:
```
YES
1 2 1 2
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
3 3
1 2
1 3
2 3
```
Output:
```
NO
```
