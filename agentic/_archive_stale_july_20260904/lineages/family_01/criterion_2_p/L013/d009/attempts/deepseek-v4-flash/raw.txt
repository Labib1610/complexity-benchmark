We can solve this by analyzing the stability condition for each link.

First, note that the operators are:

\[
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}, \quad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -4 \\ -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
\]

We need to check when \(T_u \circ T_v\) is self-adjoint. Since they commute, we can check the four possible combinations:

- \(A \circ A = A^2\)
- \(A \circ B = AB\)
- \(B \circ A = BA\)
- \(B \circ B = B^2\)

A matrix \(M\) is self-adjoint iff \(M = M^T\).

Let’s compute:

\[
A^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 1+8 & 4+20 \\ 2+10 & 8+25 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 9 & 24 \\ 12 & 33 \end{pmatrix}
\]
Not symmetric.

\[
B^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -4 \\ -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -4 \\ -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 25+8 & -20-4 \\ -10-2 & 8+1 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 33 & -24 \\ -12 & 9 \end{pmatrix}
\]
Not symmetric.

\[
AB = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -4 \\ -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 5-8 & -4+4 \\ 10-10 & -8+5 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} -3 & 0 \\ 0 & -3 \end{pmatrix}
\]
Symmetric.

\[
BA = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -4 \\ -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 5-8 & 20-20 \\ -2+2 & -8+5 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} -3 & 0 \\ 0 & -3 \end{pmatrix}
\]
Symmetric.

So the only stable combination is when the two nodes have **different** operators (one 1, one 2).  
If they have the same operator, the link is unstable.

Thus the problem reduces to:  
We have a graph, assign each node 1 or 2 such that every edge connects nodes with different labels.  
This is exactly a **bipartite graph** check. If the graph is not bipartite, output `NO`.  
If it is bipartite, we need the lexicographically smallest assignment (1 before 2).

For lexicographically smallest, we can do BFS/DFS coloring, starting with 1 for the first uncolored node, and propagate alternating colors.  
If a conflict occurs, output `NO`.

Implementation:

```python
import sys
from collections import deque

input = sys.stdin.readline

n, m = map(int, input().split())
adj = [[] for _ in range(n)]
for _ in range(m):
    u, v = map(int, input().split())
    u -= 1
    v -= 1
    adj[u].append(v)
    adj[v].append(u)

color = [-1] * n

for i in range(n):
    if color[i] == -1:
        color[i] = 0
        q = deque([i])
        while q:
            u = q.popleft()
            for v in adj[u]:
                if color[v] == -1:
                    color[v] = 1 - color[u]
                    q.append(v)
                elif color[v] == color[u]:
                    print("NO")
                    sys.exit()

print("YES")
print(" ".join(str(c + 1) for c in color))
```
