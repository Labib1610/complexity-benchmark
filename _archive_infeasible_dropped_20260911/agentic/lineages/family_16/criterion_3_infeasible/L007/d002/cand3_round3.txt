# Problem C — Impossible Deadline: Certify It

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A manufacturing facility processes **j** distinct items. The production of each item involves exactly two sequential stages: it must first undergo preparation at stage 1, and upon completion, undergo finishing at stage 2. You are provided the duration required for each stage per item. Equipment at any stage can handle at most one item at any given moment.

The facility operates for a single shift of duration **T**. All items must be completely processed before the shift ends.

You are tasked with auditing production logs from failed facilities. For each log, the required production cannot possibly be completed within the shift, regardless of how the items are ordered. Your objective is to generate a concise certificate that definitively proves the impossibility of the schedule. 

A valid certificate isolates a violation of a physical constraint. One or more violations may exist. If multiple certificates apply, print `LOAD 2` if applicable; otherwise print `LOAD 1` if applicable; otherwise print `JOB i` for the smallest applicable **i**. The possible violations are:

- `JOB i` — The **i**-th item (1-indexed) requires a sequential chain of operations whose combined duration strictly exceeds the shift length.
- `LOAD c` — The equipment at stage **c** (where **c** is 1 or 2) is assigned a total active duration across all items that strictly exceeds the shift length.

Identify and print the correct certificate that applies to the given log according to the rules above.

## Input

The first line contains two integers **j** and **T**. The number of items **j** is at least 1 and at most a warehouse capacity of 200000. The shift duration **T** is at least 1 and at most a regulatory limit of 1000000000.
Each of the following **j** lines contains exactly two integers **p1** and **p2**: the duration required for the item at stage 1 and stage 2, respectively. These durations are at least 1 and at most a machine safety shutoff limit of 1000000.

## Output

Print a single line containing the applicable certificate: either `JOB i` (where **i** is the 1-indexed item number) or `LOAD c` (where **c** is 1 or 2).

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3 100
70 60
2 2
3 4
```

**Output**
```
JOB 1
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
4 150
50 1
50 1
50 1
50 1
```

**Output**
```
LOAD 1
```
