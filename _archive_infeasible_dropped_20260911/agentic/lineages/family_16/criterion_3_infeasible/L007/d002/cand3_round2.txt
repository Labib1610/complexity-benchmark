# Problem C — Impossible Deadline: Certify It

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A manufacturing plant processes **j** distinct workpieces. Each workpiece requires processing through exactly two sequential workstations: it must first be shaped at workstation 1, and only after completion, be finished at workstation 2. You are given the processing time required at each workstation for every workpiece. A workstation can process at most one workpiece at any given time.

The plant operates during a continuous operational window of **T** minutes. All processing for all workpieces must be fully completed before this window closes.

You are auditing rejected production plans. For every plan provided, it is physically impossible to complete all processing within the operational window, no matter what schedule or ordering is used. Your task is to output a short certificate that proves why the plan is impossible.

A valid certificate pinpoints a specific bottleneck. The provided plans are structurally guaranteed to exhibit exactly one of the following violations:

- `LOAD c` — The total processing time assigned to workstation **c** (where **c** is 1 or 2) across all workpieces strictly exceeds the operational window.
- `JOB i` — The **i**-th workpiece (1-indexed) has a combined processing time across both its sequential steps that strictly exceeds the operational window.

Determine and print the single applicable certificate for the given plan.

## Input

The first line provides the number of workpieces **j** and the operational window **T**. The plant capacity ranges from 1 up to a maximum of 200000 workpieces. The operational window is at least 1 and can last up to 1000000000 minutes.
Each of the subsequent **j** lines specifies the processing times for a workpiece. It contains two integers, **p1** and **p2**, representing the time required at workstation 1 and workstation 2, respectively. Each individual processing time is at least 1 and at most 1000000 minutes.

## Output

Print a single line containing the exact applicable certificate: either `JOB i` (where `i` is the workpiece index) or `LOAD c` (where `c` is the workstation number).

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3 100
70 60
2 2
3 4
```

**Output**
```
JOB 1
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
4 150
50 1
50 1
50 1
50 1
```

**Output**
```
LOAD 1
```
