<STATEMENT>
# Conflict Resolution Certification

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

A manufacturing facility has a roster of $n$ technicians. Over time, there have been $m$ formal grievances logged. Each report is a log entry stating that technician $u$ has filed a grievance against technician $v$. However, all such grievances result in a symmetric separation: if $u$ is recorded complaining about $v$, both $u$ and $v$ are henceforth unable to work on the same shift. The management intended to divide the staff into two shifts, but the logged grievances make this impossible.

To formally document this scheduling failure, you must locate a specific obstruction: a triad of distinct technicians where every pair within the triad has triggered a symmetric separation. This means that for each of the three possible pairs of technicians in the triad, there must be at least one log entry where one technician in the pair filed a grievance against the other.

Because the number of possible three-person combinations is large, you must systematically search the grievance logs to find one. If there are multiple qualifying triads of technicians, you must report the triad that is ordered first according to technician ID. That is, when each triad's IDs are written in increasing numerical order, you must select the one that is lexicographically smallest (compare the lowest ID of the triads, then the middle ID, and finally the highest ID, selecting the triad with the smallest sequence of values).

## Input
The first line provides two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2\cdot 10^5)$), indicating the total number of technicians and the total number of grievance reports.
The following $m$ lines each contain two integers $u$ and $v$ ($1 \le u, v \le n$, $u \neq v$), representing a log entry where technician $u$ filed a grievance against technician $v$. It is guaranteed that at least one such triad of distinct technicians exists.

## Output
Output a single line containing three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$, ordered such that $a < b < c$, representing the IDs of the three technicians in the chosen triad. If multiple such triads exist, output the one that is lexicographically smallest.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
