# Problem C — Proof of Impossibility

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

All personnel at the manufacturing plant must be allocated to either the morning or the evening shift. However, friction between certain pairs of workers prevents them from sharing a shift.

Management realizes that deploying everyone across just two shifts will fail. Instead of attempting an allocation, you must provide the specific evidence that justifies this failure. Specifically, you must locate a trio of workers who are mutually incompatible—that is, the first cannot work with the second, the second cannot work with the third, and the first cannot work with the third. Finding this configuration is sufficient to prove that two shifts are inadequate. You are guaranteed that at least one such trio exists.

## Input
The first line provides two integers $n$ and $m$. The workforce size $n$ is constrained by the plant's layout, which consists of exactly 40 sections containing exactly 50 workstations each, establishing the maximum capacity. The number of friction reports $m$ is limited by the HR filing cabinet, which has exactly 400 folders, each designed to hold exactly 500 reports, setting the absolute maximum (unless the number of possible distinct worker pairs is smaller, which then serves as the limit). The guarantee that at least one mutually incompatible trio exists ensures that neither $n$ nor $m$ is less than 3.

Each of the following $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — the ID numbers of a pair of workers who cannot share a shift. Workers are identified by integers from 1 to $n$.

## Output
Output three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on a single line, ordered such that $a < b < c$. These must represent the ID numbers of a mutually incompatible trio. If more than one such trio exists, select the one that minimizes $a$. If a tie persists, minimize $b$, and finally minimize $c$ if necessary.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
