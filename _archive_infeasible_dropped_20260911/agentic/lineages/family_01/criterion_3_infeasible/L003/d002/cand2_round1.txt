# Problem C — Routing Deadlock

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

You are auditing a constellation of $n$ subspace beacons. Certain pairs of beacons are positioned such that they establish a direct, two-way data tether. 

A critical firmware bug is triggered if a "routing deadlock" exists in the constellation. A routing deadlock is formed by three distinct beacons where two of the beacons maintain a tether with each other, and the remaining beacon maintains a tether with both of those two. 

It is guaranteed that the telemetry logs contain at least one such group of three beacons. To isolate the bug, you must extract one such group.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$).
Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — a tethered pair of beacons.
It is guaranteed that at least one such group exists.

## Output
Print three integers $a$ $b$ $c$ on one line with $a < b < c$ such that among the three beacons, two of them maintain a tether with each other, and the remaining one maintains a tether with both of those two. Output the lexicographically smallest such sequence (smallest $a$; ties by smallest $b$; then smallest $c$).

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
