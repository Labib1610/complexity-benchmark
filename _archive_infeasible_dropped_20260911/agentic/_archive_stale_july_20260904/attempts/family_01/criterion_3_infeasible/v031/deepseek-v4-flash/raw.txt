We need to find a triangle in the graph where edges represent incompatibility.  
The graph is undirected, and we need the lexicographically smallest triangle.

We can do this by iterating over vertices in increasing order, and for each vertex, checking pairs of its neighbors to see if they are connected.  
Since we want the lexicographically smallest, we can break early once we find one.

Here’s the implementation:

```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
adj = [set() for _ in range(n)]
for _ in range(m):
    u, v = map(int, input().split())
    u -= 1
    v -= 1
    adj[u].add(v)
    adj[v].add(u)

for a in range(n):
    for b in adj[a]:
        if b > a:
            for c in adj[a]:
                if c > b and c in adj[b]:
                    print(a + 1, b + 1, c + 1)
                    exit()
```
