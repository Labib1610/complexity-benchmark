<STATEMENT>
# Problem C — Proof of Impossibility

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

The factory management maintains a log of incompatibility reports. Each report identifies a pair of workers who cannot share a shift. To prove that their current two-shift system is doomed to fail, management wants to find a specific pattern in the log. Your job is not to schedule the workers, but to search the log to certify this impossibility.

A "deadlock" is a selection of exactly three distinct reports such that the modulo-2 sum of their worker incidences is zero. Equivalently, across the three chosen reports, every worker who appears at all must appear an even number of times.

It is guaranteed that the log contains at least one such deadlock. Let $W$ be the set of workers who appear in the chosen reports of a deadlock. You must output the IDs of the workers in $W$. (You may deduce from the parity requirement and the uniform size of the reports that $W$ will always contain exactly three workers).

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$. The factory employs $n$ workers, where the total headcount is at least three and at most two thousand. The log contains $m$ reports, where the total number of reports is at least three, and is bounded above by two hundred thousand or the maximum number of distinct worker pairs possible for the given headcount, whichever is smaller.
Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — a report naming two incompatible workers. All reports in the log represent distinct pairs.
It is guaranteed that at least one deadlock exists in the given reports.

## Output
Print the elements of $W$ as three integers $a$, $b$, $c$ on one line with $a < b < c$. Output the lexicographically smallest sequence (smallest $a$; ties broken by smallest $b$; then smallest $c$).

## Example 1
### Input
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
### Output
1 2 3

## Example 2
### Input
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
### Output
1 2 4

This forces the model to independently deduce the triangle structure from the parity constraints and to parse the bounds from natural language.
