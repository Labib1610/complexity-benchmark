 We need to find a triangle in the graph, because a triangle (3 mutually connected nodes) cannot be 2-colored without conflict.  
The problem guarantees at least one triangle exists.  
We also need the lexicographically smallest triangle by node labels.

We can iterate over nodes in increasing order, and for each node, check pairs of its neighbors to see if they are connected.  
The first triangle found in this order will be the lexicographically smallest.

```python
n, m = map(int, input().split())
adj = [set() for _ in range(n)]
for _ in range(m):
    u, v = map(int, input().split())
    u -= 1
    v -= 1
    adj[u].add(v)
    adj[v].add(u)

for a in range(n):
    for b in adj[a]:
        if b > a:
            for c in adj[a]:
                if c > b and c in adj[b]:
                    print(a+1, b+1, c+1)
                    exit()
```
