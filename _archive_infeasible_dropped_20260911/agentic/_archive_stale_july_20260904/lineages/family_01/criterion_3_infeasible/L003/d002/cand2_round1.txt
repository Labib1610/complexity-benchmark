# Problem C — Roster Evaluation

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

The factory management team is reviewing worker interactions to pinpoint a particular type of team dysfunction. You will receive a log detailing interpersonal conflicts between specific pairs of employees. Your objective is to analyze these records and find a trilateral impasse. A trilateral impasse occurs when exactly three employees are mutually antagonistic; that is, each individual in the trio has a documented conflict with the other two members of that same trio.

Your task is to identify such a trio.

## Input
The first line provides two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$), which denote the total number of employees and the total number of documented conflicts, respectively.
Each of the subsequent $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$, representing a conflict between employee $u$ and employee $v$.

## Output
Output three space-separated integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on a single line, such that $a < b < c$. These numbers must represent the IDs of three employees forming a trilateral impasse. If multiple such trios can be found, you must provide the one that is lexicographically smallest (i.e., the one with the smallest $a$; if there is a tie, the smallest $b$; and if still tied, the smallest $c$). If no trilateral impasse exists in the records, output `0 0 0`.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
