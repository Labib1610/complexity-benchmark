# Problem C — Roster Evaluation

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

The factory management is auditing interpersonal dynamics on the assembly line. The HR database contains a list of documented disputes, where each entry involves a pair of workers who cannot be scheduled together. 

Your task is to process these logs to identify a specific three-person committee. To demonstrate a complete breakdown of communication, you must select exactly three workers such that every possible pair formed from these three individuals has a documented dispute in the database. It is guaranteed that at least one such group of three workers exists. 

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$. The workforce size $n$ is sufficient to form the required group (at least 3), but cannot exceed the building's fire safety capacity of 2000 personnel. The number of dispute logs $m$ is at least 3, and is capped by either the IT department's hard storage quota of 200,000 records or the total number of unique worker pairs, whichever is smaller.

Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — representing a pair of workers involved in a dispute.
It is guaranteed that at least one group satisfying the requirement exists.

## Output
Print three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on a single line, separated by spaces, with $a < b < c$, representing the IDs of the three workers. Every pair among $a$, $b$, and $c$ must be listed as a dispute in the input. If multiple such groups exist, output the lexicographically smallest group (the one with the smallest $a$; in case of a tie, the smallest $b$; and if still tied, the smallest $c$).

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
