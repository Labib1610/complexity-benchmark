# Problem C — Proof of Impossibility

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

Management insists on dividing the workforce into exactly two non-overlapping shifts. However, the HR department knows this plan is doomed. You must provide undeniable proof of this failure.

HR has a collection of incident files, where each file lists two specific workers who cannot be placed on the same shift. 

It is a known fact that there is at least one triad in the workforce consisting of a focal worker, a colleague they clashed with, and a third worker who has independently clashed with both of them. The presence of this configuration guarantees that two shifts are inadequate. Your task is to identify and report the members of one such triad to HR.

## Input
The first line of the log provides the workforce size $n$ and the total incident count $m$. The workforce requires a minimum of three individuals to form the configuration, while the maximum headcount is limited by the facility's strict occupancy cap of two thousand people. At least three incidents have been logged. The maximum number of incidents is restricted by the archival capacity of two hundred thousand records, or the total number of unique pairs possible among the workers, whichever is smaller. Workers are identified by integers from $1$ to $n$.

Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ ($1 \le u, v \le n$, $u \ne v$), identifying the two workers involved in a single incident.

It is guaranteed that at least one valid triad of workers exists.

## Output
Output three space-separated integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ representing the IDs of the three workers in the triad. They must be printed such that $a < b < c$.

To ensure a unique valid response when multiple such triads exist, you must select the triad that minimizes the value of $a$. If there is a tie, minimize the value of $b$ among the tied triads, and if a tie still persists, minimize $c$.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
