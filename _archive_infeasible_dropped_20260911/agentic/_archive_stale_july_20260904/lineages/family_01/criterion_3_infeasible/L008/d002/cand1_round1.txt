# Problem C — Proof of Impossibility

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

A factory's management wants to assign every worker to either the day shift or the night shift. HR is certain this scheduling is impossible and requires proof in the form of a specific group of workers.

A stack of HR incident files is provided. Each file documents an altercation between two specific staff members, meaning those two individuals must never be placed on the same shift. 

It is known that the workforce contains at least one group of exactly three workers where every possible two-person combination within that group has an incident file. Such a group mathematically guarantees that a two-shift schedule will fail. You must identify one such group.

## Input
The first line contains two integers, n and m. The staff size n is at least the minimum number of people required to form the requested group, and is capped by the factory's maximum occupancy of two thousand. The number of incident files m is at least three. It is bounded above by the lesser of two hundred thousand and the theoretical maximum number of unique two-person combinations possible among the n staff members. Staff are identified by IDs from 1 to n.

Each of the next m lines contains two integers u and v, representing the IDs of two staff members involved in a single incident. No individual is involved in an incident with themselves, and no combination of two staff members will have more than one incident file.

It is guaranteed that at least one such group of three staff members exists.

## Output
Print three integers, separated by spaces, representing the IDs of the staff members in the group. You must list them in ascending order. 

If multiple valid groups exist, you must select the one that minimizes the first ID. If there is a tie, minimize the second ID among the tied groups, and if a tie still persists, minimize the third ID.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```

</RATIONALE>
