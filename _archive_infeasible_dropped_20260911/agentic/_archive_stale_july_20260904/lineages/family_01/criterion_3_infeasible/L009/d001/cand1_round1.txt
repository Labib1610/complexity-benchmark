# Problem C — Proof of Impossibility

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

The laboratory protocol requires dividing all materials between exactly two storage units, but the provided registry of mutually volatile pairs makes this division impossible. 

Your task is to isolate a minimal certificate of this impossibility. A minimal certificate consists of exactly three materials such that no matter how they are divided between the two available units, at least two materials placed in the same unit will be volatile together. It is guaranteed that the provided registry contains at least one such certificate.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$ ($3 \le n \le 2000$, $3 \le m \le \min(n \cdot (n-1)/2, 2 \cdot 10^5)$).
Each of the next $m$ lines contains two integers $u$ and $v$ — a volatile pair.
It is guaranteed that a certificate of three materials exists.

## Output
Print three integers $a$, $b$, and $c$ on one line with $a < b < c$ representing the materials of a minimal certificate. Output the lexicographically smallest such certificate (smallest $a$; ties by smallest $b$; then smallest $c$).

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
1 2 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
5 6
1 2
1 4
2 4
3 4
3 5
4 5
```
Output:
```
1 2 4
```
