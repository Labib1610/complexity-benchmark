# Production Schedule

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

A large industrial plant divides its continuous daily operations into three distinct rotation phases: phase 1, phase 2, and phase 3. The factory floor contains exactly forty specialized workbenches. Because each technician requires a dedicated workbench, the total headcount of technicians $n$ ranges from a minimum of one person up to the maximum capacity of the floor. The technicians are identified by integers from 1 to $n$.

Over the years, management has maintained a ledger of interpersonal friction. Specific pairs of technicians have documented disputes and are strictly prohibited from being present on the factory floor during the same rotation phase. The total number of such disputes $m$ can be zero, or it might be as large as the theoretical maximum where every possible unique pair of technicians has a dispute.

Your task is to allocate every technician to exactly one of the three rotation phases. A valid allocation ensures that no two technicians involved in a mutual dispute are ever active in the same phase. Determine if such an allocation exists, and if so, produce one.

## Input
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $m$, representing the technician headcount and the number of disputes. The valid ranges for these integers are defined entirely by the physical workbench limits and the maximum possible pairs described above.
The subsequent $m$ lines each contain two integers $u$ and $v$, identifying a pair of technicians who cannot share a phase.

## Output
If there is no way to allocate the technicians without violating the rules, output a single line containing `NO`.
Otherwise, output `YES` on the first line. On the second line, output $n$ integers separated by spaces, where the $i$-th integer (which must be 1, 2, or 3) denotes the rotation phase assigned to technician $i$. Any valid allocation is acceptable.

## Example 1
Input:
```
4 5
1 2
1 3
2 3
1 4
2 4
```
Output:
```
YES
1 2 3 3
```

## Example 2
Input:
```
4 6
1 2
1 3
1 4
2 3
2 4
3 4
```
Output:
```
NO
```
