# Emergency Pressurization

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

An orbital station is composed of **n** modules, identified by integers from 1 to **n**. These modules are physically linked by **m** structural corridors. The architecture of the station guarantees that the entire facility is fully contiguous.

Due to a reactor failure, the station must operate on emergency power. There are **t** critical modules that house essential life-support systems. The commander must define a safe zone by choosing a collection of corridors to keep pressurized. The safe zone must be functionally contiguous with respect to the critical modules: it must be possible to navigate between any two critical modules exclusively by traversing the chosen pressurized corridors. Non-critical modules may be included in this safe zone to serve as transit nodes.

Pressurizing a corridor requires a specific continuous power draw. Corridors excluded from the collection are depressurized and impassable. Calculate the minimum total power draw required to maintain a collection of pressurized corridors that successfully integrates all **t** critical modules.

## Input

The first line contains three integers **n**, **m**, and **t**. The station's structural limit dictates that the module count **n** is between 1 and 200000 inclusive. The number of corridors **m** is always exactly **n** − 1. The count of critical modules **t** is between 1 and **n** inclusive.

The second line contains **t** distinct integers: the identifiers of the critical modules.

Each of the next **m** lines contains three integers **u**, **v**, and **w**. This represents a corridor linking distinct modules **u** and **v** (both between 1 and **n** inclusive) that requires a continuous power draw **w** between 1 and 1000000 inclusive to pressurize.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total power draw required to keep all critical modules functionally interconnected.
