# Orbital Station Power Grid

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

An orbital facility consists of a number of modules. Each module beyond the initial core was constructed by extending exactly one structural corridor from a previously established module. This sequential expansion ensures that the entire facility is fully contiguous.

During a low-power emergency, the station commander must power down non-essential sectors. However, specific modules house vital life-support systems. The commander must keep a selection of corridors fully pressurized so that all vital modules remain interlinked. Every vital module must be able to reach every other vital module (either directly or through intermediate modules) exclusively by traversing pressurized corridors.

Each corridor requires a certain amount of power to remain pressurized, and unpressurized corridors cannot be traversed. Determine the minimum total power required to maintain pressurization in a set of corridors that successfully connects all vital modules.

The physical and operational limits of the station are as follows:
- The facility's registry supports a module count ranging from a minimum of 1 up to a maximum of 200,000. Modules are identified by consecutive integers starting from 1.
- The total number of structural corridors is exactly determined by the station's sequential expansion method.
- The emergency protocol can designate anywhere from 1 module up to the entire station as vital.
- The power draw for pressurizing any individual corridor is an integer that is at least 1 and at most 1,000,000.

## Input

The first line contains three integers **n**, **m**, and **t**: the number of modules, the number of corridors, and the number of vital modules, respectively.
The second line contains **t** distinct integers: the identifiers of the vital modules.
Each of the next **m** lines contains three integers **u**, **v**, and **w**, representing a corridor linking distinct modules **u** and **v** that requires **w** units of power to pressurize.

All input values conform to the station's physical and operational limits described above.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total power required to keep all vital modules connected.
