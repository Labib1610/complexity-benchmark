<STATEMENT>
# Connecting the Critical Facilities

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A large industrial complex consists of $n$ facilities, numbered 1 to $n$. A specific subset of $t$ facilities are designated as critical operations centers. There are $m$ potential communication links that can be constructed between certain pairs of facilities, each requiring a specific construction cost.

Your objective is to select a subset of these links to build so that all $t$ critical facilities can communicate with one another, either directly or by routing through other intermediate facilities. You must achieve this requirement at the minimum possible total construction cost.

The complex's blueprint limits the maximum number of facilities to a strict zoning capacity of $200,000$. The construction cost of any single link is bounded by a maximum grant allowance of $1,000,000$. 

The proposed blueprint guarantees that if all $m$ potential links were built, there would be exactly one path of links between any two facilities in the complex. Consequently, the total number of proposed links $m$ is exactly one less than the total number of facilities $n$.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $n$, $m$, and $t$. The number of facilities $n$ is at least $1$ and at most the zoning capacity. The value $m$ is exactly $n - 1$. The number of critical facilities $t$ is between $1$ and $n$ inclusive.

The second line contains $t$ distinct integers: the identifiers of the critical facilities.

Each of the next $m$ lines contains three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$, representing a potential link between facility $u$ and facility $v$ with a construction cost of $w$. The facility identifiers $u$ and $v$ are between $1$ and $n$ ($u \neq v$). The cost $w$ is at least $1$ and at most the maximum grant allowance.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total cost to ensure all critical facilities are interconnected.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
5 4 1
3
1 2 4
2 3 6
3 4 2
4 5 9
```

**Output**
```
0
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 3
1 2 3
1 2 4
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
9
```

## Note

In Example 1, the critical facilities 1 and 4 are integrated by building the links that join 1 to 2, 2 to 3, and 3 to 4, for a total cost of 5 + 1 + 2 = 8. In Example 2, there is only one critical facility, so no links need to be constructed.

<RATIONALE>
The problem was modified to express the numeric bounds indirectly through scenario constraints (zoning capacity and maximum grant allowance) to implement strategy S07. The explicit "unified network" description was replaced with the mathematically equivalent graph property of "exactly one path of links between any two facilities" to force models to deduce the tree structure from its definition rather than relying on standard framing, while strictly adhering to the expert's feedback to maintain the global tree guarantee and avoid NP-hard Steiner Tree drift.
</RATIONALE>

</PRESERVED_CLASS>
