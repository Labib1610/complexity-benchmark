# Problem B — Baseline Synchronization

Time limit: 2 s
Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A facility contains $n$ sensors, labeled 1 to $n$. Absolute baselines are unobservable, so sensors must be synchronized via relative comparisons. You are given a set of $m$ potential synchronization procedures. Each procedure compares a specific pair of sensors and yields their relative offset. 

The set of $m$ procedures provides full facility coverage: any two sensors can be compared through a sequence of intermediate procedures. At the same time, the set is strictly minimal, meaning the removal of any procedure would leave some pairs of sensors unable to be compared.

You are given a target list of $t$ critical sensors. You must authorize a subset of the synchronization procedures such that all $t$ critical sensors are mutually comparable (meaning the offset between any two critical sensors can be deduced). You may authorize procedures involving non-critical sensors if they act as intermediaries to link the critical ones.

Each procedure has an associated cost. Find the minimum total cost to authorize a valid subset of procedures that synchronizes all critical sensors.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $n$, $m$, and $t$. The facility's physical capacity limits the total number of sensors $n$ to a maximum of 200000, with a minimum of 1. The minimal architecture dictates that the number of procedures $m$ is exactly one less than the number of sensors. The critical group size $t$ cannot exceed the total number of sensors $n$, and is at least 1.

The second line contains $t$ distinct integers representing the labels of the critical sensors.

The following $m$ lines each contain three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$. These indicate a potential procedure between distinct sensors $u$ and $v$ (both between 1 and $n$). The monetary cost $w$ of the procedure is at least 1 and at most 1000000. It is guaranteed that the $m$ procedures can relate all $n$ sensors as described.

## Output

Output a single integer representing the minimum possible total cost to execute the necessary procedures to deduce the offsets among all critical sensors.

## Examples


**Output**
