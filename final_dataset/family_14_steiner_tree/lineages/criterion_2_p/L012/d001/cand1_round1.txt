# Problem B — Powering the Essential Hubs

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A planetary colonization board manages $n$ outposts, which are numbered starting from 1 up to $n$. The planet's atmospheric shielding can sustain a maximum capacity of 200,000 outposts, and requires at least 1 to be active. Among these, a certain headcount $t$ are designated as **primary hubs**. This headcount is at least 1, and can include up to all $n$ outposts.

The engineering team has surveyed $m$ potential pressurized transit tubes that could be built. To maintain structural stability, the survey limits the total number of proposed tubes $m$ to exactly one less than the outpost count. Furthermore, the surveyed tubes are arranged so that every outpost can be reached from any other outpost using some sequence of tubes. 

Each tube connects two distinct outposts and requires an investment of $w$ credits. The cost $w$ requires a minimum investment of 1 credit, and is strictly capped by the planetary bank's maximum transaction limit of 1,000,000 credits.

You must choose a subset of the surveyed tubes to build so that all primary hubs are interconnected (either directly or via other outposts). Determine the minimum total investment required.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $n$, $m$, and $t$. Their bounds and relationships are defined by the atmospheric constraints, network properties, and hub rules described above.
The second line contains $t$ distinct integers: the ids of the primary hubs.
Each of the next $m$ lines contains three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$: a surveyed tube joining distinct outposts $u$ and $v$ at an investment cost of $w$ credits. 

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total investment required to interconnect all primary hubs.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 3 2
1 4
1 2 5
2 3 1
3 4 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
5 4 1
3
1 2 4
2 3 6
3 4 2
4 5 9
```

**Output**
```
0
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 3
1 2 3
1 2 4
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
9
```

## Note

In Example 1, the primary hubs 1 and 4 can only be joined by building the tubes through outposts 2 and 3, so all three surveyed tubes (5 + 1 + 2 = 8) must be built. In Example 2, there is only one primary hub, so no tubes need to be built.
