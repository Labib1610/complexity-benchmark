# Subterranean Mycelial Network

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

Deep beneath a forest floor lies a vast mycelial network consisting of $R$ fungal colonies. These colonies are linked by $S$ bidirectional hyphal threads. These threads connect all $R$ colonies together, forming a single, interconnected subterranean web.

Biologists have identified $P$ priority colonies that produce a rare medical enzyme. To study their symbiotic interactions effectively, the research team must isolate a unified observation sector.

You are tasked with designating a subset of the $R$ colonies to serve as this observation sector. Your selection must adhere to the following rules:
1. The sector must contain all $P$ of the priority colonies. You are free to include any number of additional non-priority colonies to satisfy the next rule.
2. The chosen colonies must be fully contiguous. Specifically, for any two colonies in your chosen subset, there must exist a sequence of hyphal threads connecting them such that every colony along the sequence is also part of the chosen subset.

When two colonies that share a hyphal thread are both included in the observation sector, that thread becomes "active" and demands a specific nutrient upkeep to sustain. Threads where at least one endpoint is excluded from the observation sector remain dormant and demand no nutrient upkeep.

Your task is to determine the minimum possible total nutrient upkeep of the active threads required to establish a valid observation sector.

## Input

The first line contains three integers $R$, $S$, and $P$. 
The total number of colonies $R$ satisfies $1 \le R \le 200000$. 
The number of hyphal threads $S$ is exactly equal to $R - 1$. 
The number of priority colonies $P$ satisfies $1 \le P \le R$.

The second line contains $P$ distinct integers representing the identifiers of the priority colonies.

Each of the following $S$ lines contains three integers $u$, $v$, and $w$ ($1 \le u, v \le R$, $u \neq v$). This indicates a bidirectional hyphal thread between colony $u$ and colony $v$ with a nutrient upkeep of $w$ ($1 \le w \le 1000000$). 

## Output

Output a single integer representing the minimum total nutrient upkeep of the active threads for a valid observation sector.
