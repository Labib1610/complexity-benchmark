# Facility Network Provisioning

Time limit: 2 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A facility houses a number of server racks. The network is pre-wired with fiber conduits. To prevent routing loops, the physical wiring guarantees that data can travel between any two racks via exactly one sequence of conduits. Each conduit requires a specific activation fee to be turned on.

A corporate tenant requires a specific subset of the racks to be fully interconnected. You must choose a set of conduits to activate such that all of the tenant's designated racks can communicate with one another. When data travels between two designated racks, it may pass through other non-designated racks, which will function as free pass-through switches provided the conduits along the route are activated. 

Your task is to determine the minimum total activation fee required to establish communication among all of the tenant's designated racks.

## Input

The first line contains three integers representing the total number of racks, the total number of pre-wired conduits, and the number of designated racks. The facility capacity limits the total number of racks to a maximum of 200000. The number of designated racks is at least 1 and at most the total number of racks. The number of conduits is exactly one less than the total number of racks.

The second line contains the distinct integers representing the identifiers of the designated racks.

Each of the subsequent lines describes a pre-wired conduit with three integers: the two different racks it connects (each validly numbered from 1 up to the total number of racks), and its activation fee. The activation fee is at least 1 and is subject to a maximum cap of 1000000.

## Output

Print a single integer representing the minimum total activation fee to establish the required communication.

