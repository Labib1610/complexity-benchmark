# Problem A — Linking the Priority Sites

Time limit: 5 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A research complex consists of **n** sites (numbered 1 to n). Certain pairs of sites can be connected by a conduit at a specific cost. Among these sites, **t** are designated as **priority** sites. All priority sites must ultimately be connected to one another through the conduits you choose to build (this can be direct, or routed through other sites). The remaining sites are optional relays: you may route your network through them if it reduces the overall cost, or you may bypass them entirely.

Determine a selection of conduits that connects all priority sites at the absolute minimum total cost, and output that minimum cost.

## Input

The first line contains three integers **n**, **m**, and **t**. The site count **n** is at least 1, and restricted by the complex's grid layout to a maximum of 100. The segment count **m** is between 0 and the theoretical maximum of n·(n−1)/2. The priority count **t** is at least 1, and strictly capped at 10 by the stock of primary transceivers.

The second line contains **t** distinct integers: the identifiers of the priority sites.

Each of the next **m** lines contains three integers **u**, **v**, and **w**. The identifiers **u** and **v** are distinct integers from 1 to **n**, representing the endpoints of a conduit. The cost **w** is at least 1 and cannot exceed the maximum hardware grant of 1000000.

The available conduits are guaranteed to be sufficient to connect all priority sites.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total cost to connect all priority sites.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 6 3
1 2 3
1 4 1
2 4 1
3 4 1
1 2 2
2 3 2
1 3 2
```

**Output**
```
3
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
4 4 3
1 2 3
1 2 1
2 3 1
1 4 10
3 4 10
```

**Output**
```
2
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 1
1
1 2 5
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
0
```

## Note

In Example 1, routing conduits through the optional relay site 4 (costs 1+1+1 = 3) is cheaper than connecting the three priority sites directly (which would cost 4). In Example 3, there is only 1 priority site, so no conduit is needed.
