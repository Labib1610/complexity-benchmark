# Problem A — Subterranean Expedition

Time limit: 5 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A geological expedition is exploring a vast subterranean system of caverns. The system consists of **n** distinct caverns, uniquely identified by numbers starting from 1 up to **n**. The expedition team has identified **m** narrow passages that could potentially be cleared to allow travel between specific pairs of caverns. Clearing a passage requires a certain amount of physical effort, measured in energy units.

The team has stationed a group of **t** lead scientists in specific caverns to conduct long-term studies. Each scientist occupies exactly one cavern. The remaining caverns do not have scientists stationed in them, but they can still be used as empty transit hubs.

To coordinate their findings, all **t** scientists must be able to travel to each other's caverns. This means there must be a continuous route of cleared passages connecting any scientist's cavern to any other scientist's cavern. They can pass through the empty transit caverns along the way.

The expedition wants to minimize the total effort spent on clearing passages while ensuring that all scientists can reach one another. Find the minimum total energy required.

## Input

The first line provides three integers: **n**, **m**, and **t**.
- The count of scientists **t** is a positive integer no larger than the number of sides on a decagon.
- The count of caverns **n** is a positive integer no larger than the square of the maximum possible value for **t**.
- The count of potential passages **m** is a non-negative integer no larger than the number of unique pairs of caverns.

The second line contains **t** distinct integers: the identifiers of the caverns occupied by the scientists.

Each of the next **m** lines contains three integers **u**, **v**, and **w**. This indicates a potential passage between cavern **u** and cavern **v** (**u** ≠ **v**) that requires **w** energy units to clear.
- The cavern identifiers **u** and **v** are between 1 and **n** inclusive.
- The energy **w** is a positive integer no larger than the square of one thousand.

It is guaranteed that the available passages are sufficient to ensure all scientists can reach one another.

## Output

Print a single integer: the minimum total energy required to clear a set of passages that allows all scientists to reach one another.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
4 6 3
1 2 3
1 4 1
2 4 1
3 4 1
1 2 2
2 3 2
1 3 2
```

**Output**
```
3
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
4 4 3
1 2 3
1 2 1
2 3 1
1 4 10
3 4 10
```

**Output**
```
2
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3 2 1
1
1 2 5
2 3 5
```

**Output**
```
0
```

## Note

In Example 1, clearing passages through the empty transit cavern 4 (energy 1+1+1 = 3) requires less effort than directly connecting the three scientists' caverns (which would require 2+2 = 4). In Example 3, there is only one scientist, so no passages need to be cleared.
