# Problem A — Unacquainted Cohort

Time limit: 5 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A digital community consists of **n** members (identified by numbers 1 through **n**). Within this community, there are **m** established mutual connections. Each connection links a pair of members who interact with each other.

A cohort of members is defined as **completely unacquainted** if no two members within the cohort share a connection (there is zero interaction between any pair of them).

You are provided with a desired cohort size **k**. Your task is to verify whether a completely unacquainted cohort of exactly **k** members can be formed, and if so, to provide one such cohort.

## Input

The first line of the input provides three integers **n**, **m**, and **k**.
The values adhere to these limits:
- **n** is at least 1. The community platform's database allocates exactly 50 slots in total. Exactly 4 of these slots are permanently assigned to automated moderation bots, and the remaining slots dictate the strict upper limit for the value of **n**.
- **m** is at least 0 and bounded above by the total number of distinct pairs that can be formed from the **n** members.
- **k** is at least 1 and at most **n**.

The subsequent **m** lines each contain two integers **u** and **v** (where **u** and **v** are between 1 and **n** inclusive, and **u** ≠ **v**), representing a mutual connection between member **u** and member **v**. No connection is duplicated in the input.

## Output

If a completely unacquainted cohort of exactly **k** members can be assembled, print `YES` on the initial line. On the subsequent line, print the **k** distinct member identifiers that make up this cohort, separated by spaces. Any valid cohort satisfying the condition is accepted.

If it is impossible to form such a cohort, print `NO`.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```text
4 4 3
1 2
1 3
2 3
3 4
```

**Output**
```text
NO
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```text
5 2 3
1 2
3 4
```

**Output**
```text
YES
1 3 5
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```text
3 3 1
1 2
2 3
1 3
```

**Output**
```text
YES
1
```

## Note

A completely unacquainted cohort requires that absolutely no two chosen members have a mutual connection. In Example 2, members 1, 3, and 5 have no connections among them. In Example 1, it is impossible to find three members out of the four who are all mutually unconnected.
