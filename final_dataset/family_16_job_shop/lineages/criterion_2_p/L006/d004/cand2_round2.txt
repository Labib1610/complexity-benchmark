# Problem B — Pipeline Installation

Time limit: 1 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

You are designing a dual-layer pipeline consisting of **j** sections. Each section requires a ground-level rigid pipe of length **p1** and an elevated flexible hose of length **p2**.

You must arrange all **j** sections into a single sequence from left to right. Let the chosen sequence of sections be placed into positions $1, 2, \dots, j$, where $p1_i$ and $p2_i$ are the lengths for the section assigned to position $i$.

The layout of the installation along the x-axis is determined as follows:
- The ground-level rigid pipes are laid end-to-end with no gaps. Rigid pipe $i$ begins exactly where rigid pipe $i-1$ ends. The first rigid pipe begins at x-coordinate 0.
- The elevated flexible hoses are also laid end-to-end, but with constraints to connect with the ground level. Flexible hose $i$ begins at an x-coordinate exactly equal to the greater of two values: the x-coordinate where its corresponding rigid pipe $i$ ends, and the x-coordinate where flexible hose $i-1$ ends. (The first flexible hose begins exactly where the first rigid pipe ends).

The overall span of your installation is the exact x-coordinate where the $j$-th flexible hose ends. 

Find an ordering of the sections that makes their overall span as small as possible, and report this smallest possible span.

## Input

The first line contains a single integer **j** (1 ≤ j ≤ 200000).
Each of the next **j** lines contains two integers **p1** and **p2** (1 ≤ p1, p2 ≤ 1000000): the length of the rigid pipe and the length of the flexible hose for a section.

## Output

Print a single integer: the smallest possible overall span.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3
3 2
2 4
1 3
```

**Output**
```
10
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
1
5 3
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3
2 2
2 2
2 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

## Note

In Example 1, an optimal arrangement places the sections in the order of the 3rd, 2nd, and 1st from the input.
- The 3rd input section (lengths 1 and 3) has its rigid pipe from x=0 to 1, and its flexible hose from x=1 to 4.
- The 2nd input section (lengths 2 and 4) has its rigid pipe from x=1 to 3, and its flexible hose from x=4 to 8.
- The 1st input section (lengths 3 and 2) has its rigid pipe from x=3 to 6, and its flexible hose from x=8 to 10.
The overall span is 10.
