<STATEMENT>
# Problem B — Exhibition Layout

Time limit: 1 s   Memory limit: 256 MB

## Description

A city is planning the layout of a linear exhibition along a single avenue. The exhibition will consist of **j** pavilions. Each pavilion has a fixed Indoor section of length **t1** and a fixed Outdoor section of length **t2**.

You must decide the sequence in which the **j** pavilions are ordered. Let your chosen sequence be $P_1, P_2, \dots, P_j$.

The placement of these sections along the avenue (represented as a positive coordinate axis) follows strict zoning rules:
- All Indoor sections are built contiguously. The Indoor section of $P_1$ begins at coordinate 0. For all $k > 1$, the Indoor section of $P_k$ begins exactly where the Indoor section of $P_{k-1}$ ends.
- The Outdoor section of $P_1$ begins exactly at the coordinate where the Indoor section of $P_1$ ends.
- For all $k > 1$, the Outdoor section of $P_k$ begins exactly at the coordinate where the Indoor section of $P_k$ ends, or at the coordinate where the Outdoor section of $P_{k-1}$ ends, whichever coordinate is greater.
- The length occupied by any section on the axis is exactly its given **t1** or **t2** value.

Your goal is to choose the sequence of pavilions that minimizes the ending coordinate of the Outdoor section of the final pavilion, $P_j$. Output this minimum possible ending coordinate.

## Input

The first line contains a single integer **j** (1 ≤ j ≤ 200000).
Each of the next **j** lines contains exactly 2 integers **t1** and **t2** (1 ≤ t1, t2 ≤ 1000000): the length of the Indoor section and the length of the Outdoor section of a pavilion.

## Output

Print a single integer: the smallest possible ending coordinate of the final pavilion's Outdoor section.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**
```
3
3 2
2 4
1 3
```

**Output**
```
10
```

### Example 2

**Input**
```
1
5 3
```

**Output**
```
8
```

### Example 3

**Input**
```
3
2 2
2 2
2 2
```

**Output**
```
8
```

## Note

In Example 2, there is only one pavilion. Its Indoor section has length 5, starting at 0 and ending at 5. Its Outdoor section has length 3, starting at 5 and ending at 8. The final ending coordinate is 8.
